## Email Writing Strategies

### 三大信件類型

1. **提出建議 — Making a Recommendation**
2. **發出邀請 — Extending an Invitation**
3. **說明情況或請求協助 — Sharing Information / Proposing a Solution**

### 信件結構

1. 稱呼敬語 (Dear …)
2. 開頭禮貌問候
3. 說明目的
4. 展開問題
5. 提出請求
6. 結尾禮貌致意

## 重要句型 (Important Sentence Patterns)

**1. …cannot emphasize the importance of … too much**（再怎麼強調…的重要性也不為過）

- We cannot emphasize the importance of protecting the natural environment against being devastated too much.

---

**2. Nothing is + more + adj. + than to + V**（沒有比…更重要的事情了）

- For parents, nothing is more important than to educate children in a proper manner.

---

**3. …the most + adj. + noun + (that) + S + have ever + seen/known/heard**（…是迄今最…的…）

- A paucity of interpersonal skills is one of the most serious defects that we have ever known about today's college students.

---

**4. There is no denying that + S + V**（無可否認的…）

- There is no denying that the practicality of our tertiary education has gone from bad to worse.

---

**5. It is universally acknowledged that + S + V**（全世界都知道…）

- It is universally acknowledged that it is the doctor's duty and obligation to heal the wounded and rescue the dying.

---

**6. There is no doubt that + S + V**（毫無疑問的…）

- There is no doubt that the Internet is far more convenient and efficient than the written words or other conventional means of conveying meaning.

---

**7. An advantage of … is that + S + V**（…的優點是…）

- An advantage of children's participation in some paid work is that this practice can cultivate their independence, self-determination and sense of responsibility.

---

**8. The reason why + S + V … is that + S + V**（…的原因是…）

- The reason why a large number of customs have altered substantially is that most of them are associated with some superstitious beliefs, and they are incompatible with this world with highly-developed technology.

---

**9. So + adj. + be + S + that + S + V**（如此…以致於…）

- So pervasive are advertisements that no one can avoid being influenced by them.

---

**10. Adj. + as + S + be, S + V**（雖然…）

- Useful and convenient as cellphones are, it is by no means appropriate for adolescents to indulge in them.
- (by no means = in no way = on no account — 一點也不…)

---

**11. The more … S + V, the more … S + V**（愈…愈…）

- The more children are encouraged to get in touch with various aspects of society, the more comprehensive their knowledge will be.

---

**12. By + Ving, … can …**（藉由…, …能夠…）

- By putting the responsibility into the government's hand, citizens can enjoy a higher level of assurance in quality.

---

**13. … enable + O + to + V**（使…能夠…）

- Community service programs enable the government to assuage the strain on its limited budget.

---

**14. On no account can we + V**（我們絕對不能…）

- On no account can we ignore the significance of education.

---

**15. It is high/right time + S + 過去式**（該是…的時候了）

- It is high time we evaluated the roles that modern technology plays in our life from more balanced and objective perspectives.

## Sample Email — Apartment Repairs

**Prompt:** Your landlord hasn't fixed problems with your apartment. Write an email requesting repairs.

> To: Mr. Thompson
> Subject: Request for apartment repairs
>
> Dear Mr. Thompson,
>
> I hope this message finds you well. I am writing to inform you about several problems I have encountered since moving into the apartment.
>
> First, the heating system is not functioning properly and often stops working, which makes the apartment exceedingly freezing, particularly at night. Furthermore, there are cracks in the roof, and during rainy days, water leaks into the room.
>
> These issues have substantially influenced my concentration on my studies. On account of the cold temperature as well as the noise and water dripping from the ceiling, I find it annoying and difficult to be immersed in my schoolwork. Actually, this situation has become so unbearable that I had to stay overnight at my friend's house.
>
> I would greatly appreciate it if you could swiftly make arrangements to address these problems thoroughly since I enjoy dwelling in this area and hope these hassles could be eradicated and make it more hospitable.
>
> Thank you a million for your consideration and care.
>
> Yours Faithfully,
> Andrew

## Email Templates — Key Phrases

### 諮詢信 (Inquiry Letter)

- I would be much obliged to you if you could let me know the procedures I have to go through.
- Your prompt and favorable attention to my inquiry would be highly appreciated.
- I am looking forward to a favorable reply/response at your earliest convenience.
- Would you provide me with some valuable advice?
- Your kind reply will be highly appreciated.
- I am writing to enquire whether I may become a member of your club.
- I would be grateful if you could let me know your annual fee charges and any other information which would be useful.
- Please let me know as soon as possible how you propose to settle this matter.

### 投訴信 (Complaint Letter)

- I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with…
- There are some problems with… that I wish to bring to your attention.
- I regret to inform you that…
- You can imagine my disappointment when I discovered that…
- I hope that the authorities concerned will consider my suggestions and improve the situation as best as they can.
- We trust that you will now consider this matter seriously and make an effort to prevent the recurrence of this kind.
- We will appreciate your willingness to make up for the loss.
