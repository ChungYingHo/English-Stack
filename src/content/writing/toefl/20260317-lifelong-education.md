## 學術討論寫作要點

- 確實於討論中加入自己的觀點，而非僅僅重複對方論點
- 字數至少 100 字；可選擇同意，但必須增添自己的支持內容
- 立場可採折衷觀點，學術討論問題並無「正確」答案
- 完善的回應包含明確適當的理由、例子和細節
- 句型結構與詞彙的品質與準確性是評分標準之一，切勿使用非正式文句

---

## 論文段落結構

1. **Introduction**: Background → Statement of the issue → Thesis Statement
2. **Body Paragraphs**: Topic Sentence → Supporting Sentences → Illustration/Examples → Concession/Transition
3. **Conclusion**: Restate thesis → Restate ideas → Prediction

## Academic Discussion — Lifelong Learning

**Dr. Diaz:**
In today's context, there's a growing consensus that education should extend beyond high school and college, encompassing lifelong learning. Considering the shift towards lifelong education, which field of study do you believe adults should prioritize for their continuous learning journey?

**Andrew:**
In my opinion, it's important for adults to have the opportunity to take foreign language classes as part of their lifelong education. Learning a new language can open doors to different cultures, enhance communication skills, and boost career prospects. Plus, it's a way to keep our minds active and engaged throughout our lives. So, I believe offering foreign language courses to adults is a valuable way to support their continuous learning journey.

**Claire:**
I think art classes should definitely be available to adults as part of lifelong education. Engaging in art allows us to express ourselves, relieve stress, and tap into our creativity. Even if someone didn't have the chance to explore art when they were younger, providing these classes to adults can offer them a way to discover new talents, develop new hobbies, and continue growing personally, no matter their age.

:::tip
**建議結構：** 立場表明 → 回應學生觀點 → 理由陳述 → 自我觀點 → 解釋理由 → 例證說明
:::

## Academic Discussion — Soft Skills in Education（2023/09/03 真題）

**Dr. Diaz:**
This week, we'll examine issues in education reform. Some educators think that by focusing mainly on foundational knowledge in subjects like reading, math, and science, schools are not fully preparing students for success in the workplace. These educators argue that soft skills — nonacademic skills such as being empathetic or having a strong work ethic — should be part of the curriculum, with classroom time dedicated to developing these skills. Do you agree? Why or why not?

**Andrew:**
I agree. While students need a strong foundation in academic subjects, soft skills are important because they affect how well students do their work. If classroom time is set aside for these skills, students will take them seriously and their academic work will benefit. Teachers could design lessons so that students learn about a skill and then do role-playing activities with classmates.

**Claire:**
Soft skills are important, but I don't agree with using classroom time to work on them. I'm not sure how teachers would measure students' progress in developing these skills. If students' progress can't be measured on something taught, I'm not sure if there's any point to teaching.

---

**Sample Response:**

Undoubtedly while rudimentary core academic subjects lay the groundwork for academic proficiency, from my perspective, a portion of classroom time should be committed to cultivating soft skills as these are pivotal in helping students adapt to an ever-changing job market. I espouse Andrew's opinion. Explicitly, the inclusion of soft skills in the curriculum is imperative for holistic student development. The classroom serves as the microcosm of the broader world, abounding with plenty of people with disparate attributes, personalities, and backgrounds, and challenges, making it the optimal surroundings to nurture nonacademic techniques. To illustrate, the very nature of group projects prompts critical thinking, communication, collaboration, teamwork, mutual esteem, conflict resolution, adaptability and problem-solving capabilities. These are not just abstract concepts but tangible skills.

Moreover, soft skills and academic knowledge are not mutually exclusive; rather, they complement each other. Proficient communication abilities allow students to establish a study rapport with their peers and professors, fostering the surroundings conducive to learning. When encountering impediments to academic attainment, students can seek assistance from their classmates and instructors more efficaciously. Furthermore, strong collaboration skills facilitate cohesive problem-solving efforts, enabling students to approach issues from various perspectives and garner both theoretical knowledge and real-life techniques in tandem. With these soft skills inculcated one can overshadow other rivals, ultimately boosting their competitiveness in the future capricious work settings.

Take a moment to reflect on leaders like Barack Obama or Oprah Winfrey. Albeit they are undoubtedly informative as well as erudite in their distinctive domains, it is their phenomenal communication and interpersonal skills that set them apart as well as distinguished. Hence, by seamlessly blending soft skill development into the curriculum through activities like group discussions or debates, we can help students not just accomplish attain academic accomplishment but, more importantly, be equipped with competence to tide over a myriad of life's challenges and break through predicaments.

In this manner, I hold the notion that soft skills should be incorporated into a school curriculum due to the fact that this can be beneficial for preparing students for the complexities and demands of a rapidly evolving job market.

## TOEFL Writing Score Guide

- **5 分**: 文章切題，闡述充分，說服力強；段落組織緊湊，過渡自然；句子連接順暢，句法嫻熟靈活；用詞準確。文章可能有個別語法拼寫錯誤，但不影響整體理解。
- **4 分**: 文章切題，闡述基本充分，在某些地方有小漏洞；段落組織緊湊；用詞基本準確；有少量用詞不當和語法錯誤。
- **3 分**: 文章切題，闡述尚可，略顯空洞；段落結構尚可；有部分句法使用不當；語法及拼寫有若干錯誤。
- **2 分**: 文章切題；語言表達有較大缺陷；句子間聯繫鬆散，詞不達意；語法錯誤較多，拼寫錯誤多。
- **1 分**: 文章切題，但闡述空洞；段落組織混亂；語法與詞彙錯誤較多。
- **0 分**: 文章在結構與語言上都有嚴重錯誤，英語水準極低。
