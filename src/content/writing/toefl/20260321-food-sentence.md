## Email 寫作要領

撰寫電子郵件時，需具備以下五個核心元素：

1. **稱呼敬語** — Dear …
2. **開頭表明目的** — I am writing regarding…
3. **陳述問題** — What happened…
4. **提出請求** — Would you please…
5. **結尾禮貌致意** — Thanks for…

### 評分標準

| 評分項目 | 說明 |
|----------|------|
| Communicative Goal | 是否完成溝通目的，語氣得體有禮 |
| Organization & Cohesion | 是否組織清晰邏輯連接，句子銜接自然 |
| Lexical Resources | 詞彙是否精確、自然、多元變化 |
| Grammatical Range & Accuracy | 語法範圍精確 |
| Mechanics | 拼寫、標點、格式是否符合規範 |

## Email Practice — 求助信 (Request/Help Letter)

### 主體段常用句型

- seek/ask for one's assistance/help in sth.
- turn to sb.
- formally request sb. to do sth.
- look after/take care of sb./sth. during one's absence
- Would you be so kind to…?
- I wonder if you could spare some time to attend to my home while I am away for my annual paid holiday.
- I would appreciate it if you could…
- drop in (on)/by sb.
- take the trouble to do sth.
- the operation instructions/guidebook
- every other day / every two days
- Your timely help is of great significance to me.
- Sth. is of great value to me because it is a gift from sb. who…

### 結尾段常用句型

- I would be greatly grateful/thankful to you if you could lend me a hand at such a critical time and I am looking forward to hearing from you.
- If you have any information, please feel free to email me or call me anytime.
- Should anything happen, please call me, and I would be available all the time.
- A favorable reply at your earliest convenience is highly appreciated.
- Thank you for your attention to these requests.
- I shall remain grateful to you for the great help.

## Writing Practice

**Prompt:**

You will move to a new city for work. You know some people who live there.

In your letter:
- Ask them for help finding accommodation
- Tell them where you would like to live
- Tell them the type of place you are looking for

**Sample Response:**

> Dear Sir/Madam,
>
> It has been a while since our last discourse. How are you doing? I have some good news: in the near future, my family and I will be moving to the city where you dwell, and we could meet more frequently. I would like to ask for your assistance with the relocation. Acquiring sufficient and exhaustive advice with respect to the optimal places to inhabit with a family would be crucial. The accommodation I am questing for should be equipped with three bedrooms and be well-lit. I enjoy spacious and bright homes with a large kitchen.
>
> Ideally, I would like to live in the vicinity of where I am going to be working, or in the downtown district. The location with ample amenities is my priority. If possible, there should be some shops nearby, like a supermarket; meanwhile, some recreational facilities, such as parks or gyms enhancing quality of life cannot be neglected.
>
> Additionally, I have always dreamed of having a large balcony or yard to plant a variety of beautiful flowers. Nonetheless despite accessible transport, a private parking lot is a must-have for us. Moreover, the education system in the area plays an essential role in choosing a place to live since we have a child. I know your advice is always helpful; therefore, I hope this time, relying on your opinion, we will find a gorgeous place for our family.
>
> We appreciate your kindness.
>
> Faithfully yours,
> Kenny
