## Background for the Industrial Revolution

---

### Paragraph 1

The Industrial Revolution had several roots, one of which was a commercial revolution that, beginning as far back as the sixteenth century, accompanied Europe's expansion overseas. Both exports and imports showed spectacular growth, particularly in England and France. An increasingly larger portion of the stepped-up commercial activity was the result of trade with overseas colonies. Imports included a variety of new beverages, spices, and ship's goods around the world and brought money flowing back. Europe's economic institutions, particularly those in England, were strong, had wealth available for new investment, and seemed almost to be waiting for some technological breakthrough that would expand their profit-making potential even more.

<details>
<summary>翻譯 Translation</summary>

工業革命有好幾個根源，其中之一是一場「商業革命」，它早在十六世紀就已經開始，並伴隨著歐洲向海外擴張。出口與進口都呈現驚人的成長，尤其是在英格蘭與法國。在這場日益增加的商業活動中，越來越大的比例來自與海外殖民地的貿易。進口物資包括各種新飲料、香料以及來自世界各地的船貨，也讓金錢源源不絕地回流到歐洲。歐洲的經濟體制，尤其是英格蘭的，根基深厚、擁有可供新投資的財富，幾乎像是在等待某種技術突破，好讓他們的獲利潛力更上一層樓。

### Paragraph 2

The breakthrough came in Great Britain, where several economic advantages created a climate especially favorable to the encouragement of new technology. One was its geographic location at the crossroads of international trade. Internally, Britain was endowed with easily navigable natural waterways, which helped its trade and communication with the world. Beginning in the 1770's, it enjoyed a boom in canal building, which helped make its domestic market more accessible. Because water transportation was the cheapest means of carrying goods to market, canals reduced prices and thus increased consumer demand. Great Britain also had rich deposits of coal that fed the factories springing up in industrial and consumer goods.

<details>
<summary>翻譯 Translation</summary>

突破發生在英國——這裡有幾項經濟優勢，營造出特別有利於新技術發展的環境。其一是它位於國際貿易十字路口的地理位置。在國內，英國擁有得天獨厚、容易通航的天然水道，協助它與世界進行貿易與通訊。從 1770 年代開始，它經歷了一波運河興建熱潮，這讓國內市場彼此更為連通。由於水運是把貨物送到市場最便宜的方式，運河壓低了價格，因此提高了消費者需求。英國也擁有豐富的煤礦蘊藏，能餵養各種如雨後春筍般冒出的工業與消費財工廠。

</details>

### Paragraph 3

Another advantage was Britain's large population of rural, agricultural wage earners, as well as cottage workers, who had the potential of being more mobile than peasants of some other countries. Eventually they found their way to the cities or mining communities and provided the human power upon which the Industrial Revolution was built. The British people were also consumers; the absence of internal tariffs, such as those that existed in France or Italy or between the German states, made Britain the largest free-trade area in Europe. Britain's relatively stable government also helped create an atmosphere conducive to industrial progress.

<details>
<summary>翻譯 Translation</summary>

另一項優勢是英國擁有大量的鄉村農業工資勞工，以及在自家手工生產的「家庭工人」——這些人比某些其他國家的農民更具備「移動」的潛力。他們最終湧入城市或礦業社群，提供了支撐工業革命所需的人力。英國人也是消費者；它沒有像法國、義大利或德意志各邦之間那種「內部關稅」，使得英國成為當時歐洲最大的自由貿易區。英國相對穩定的政府，也協助營造出有利於工業進步的氛圍。

</details>

### Paragraph 4

Great Britain's better-developed banking and credit system also helped speed the industrial progress, as did the fact that it was the home of an impressive array of entrepreneurs and inventors. Among them were a large number of nonconformists whose religious principles encouraged thrift and industry rather than luxurious living and who tended to pour their profits back into their business, thus providing the basis for continued expansion.

<details>
<summary>翻譯 Translation</summary>

英國發展得較完善的銀行與信用體系，也協助加快工業的進展。同時，英國本身也擁有一大群令人印象深刻的企業家與發明家。當中有許多「非國教徒（nonconformists）」——他們的宗教原則鼓勵節儉與勤奮，而非奢華生活，他們也傾向把獲利再投入到自己的事業，因而為事業持續擴張奠定基礎。

### Paragraph 5

A precursor to the Industrial Revolution was a revolution in agricultural techniques. Ideas about agricultural reform developed first in Holland, where as early as the mid-seventeenth century, such modern methods as crop rotation, heavy fertilization, and diversification were all in use. Dutch peasant farmers were known throughout Europe for their agricultural innovations, but as British markets and opportunities grew, the English quickly learned from them. As early as the seventeenth century the Dutch were helping them drain marshes and fens where, with the help of advanced techniques, they grew new crops. By the mid-eighteenth century new agricultural methods as well as selective breeding of livestock had caught on throughout the country.

<details>
<summary>翻譯 Translation</summary>

工業革命的先聲，是一場「農業技術上的革命」。農業改革的思維最早是在荷蘭發展出來的——早在十七世紀中葉，輪作、密集施肥與多樣化耕作這些現代方法就已經被廣泛採用。荷蘭農民以農業創新聞名全歐洲，而隨著英國市場與機會擴大，英國人很快就向他們學習。早在十七世紀，荷蘭人就在英國協助他們排乾沼澤與低濕地——並借助先進技術在這些土地上種植新作物。到了十八世紀中葉，新的耕作方法以及對牲畜的「選擇性育種」已在全國蔚為風潮。

</details>

### Paragraph 6

Much of the increased production was consumed by Great Britain's burgeoning population. At the same time, people were moving to the city, partly because of the enclosure movement; that is, the fencing of common fields and pastures in order to provide more compact, efficient privately held agricultural parcels that would produce more goods and greater profits. In the sixteenth century enclosures were usually used for creating sheep pastures, but by the eighteenth century new farming techniques made it advantageous for large landowners to seek enclosures in order to improve agricultural production. Between 1714 and 1820 over 6 million acres of English land were enclosed. (A)■ As a result, many small, independent farmers were forced to sell out simply because they could not compete. (B)■ Non-landholding peasants and cottage workers, who worked for wages and grazed cows or pigs on the village common, were also hurt when the common was no longer available. (C)■ It was such people who began to flock to the cities seeking employment and who found work in the factories that would transform the nation and, the world. (D)■

<details>
<summary>翻譯 Translation</summary>

增加出來的產量，大多被英國急速膨脹的人口所消耗。同時間，人們也正陸續遷往城市——部分原因是「圈地運動（enclosure movement）」：把原本共有的田地與牧場圍起來，變成更集中、更有效率的私有農地，以生產更多商品並獲取更高利潤。在十六世紀，圈地通常被用來闢設綿羊牧場；但到了十八世紀，新的耕作技術使得大地主透過圈地來改善農業生產更有利可圖。1714 到 1820 年之間，英國有超過 600 萬英畝的土地被圈占。(A)■ 結果，許多小型獨立農民因為無法競爭而被迫賣掉土地。(B)■ 那些沒有土地、靠工資維生、把牛或豬養在村莊公地上的農民與家庭手工業者，也因為公地不再存在而受到傷害。(C)■ 正是這些人開始湧入城市尋找工作，並在後來改變整個國家、乃至全世界的工廠裡找到了職位。(D)■
