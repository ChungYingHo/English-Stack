**[Paragraph 4]** Yet another scheme for approximating Earth's age had been proposed in 1715 by Sir Edmund Halley (1656–1742), whose name we associate with the famous comet. Halley surmised that the ocean formed soon after the origin of the planet and therefore would be only slightly younger than the age of the solid Earth. He reasoned that the original ocean was not salty and that subsequently salt derived from the weathering of rocks was brought to the sea by streams. Thus, if one knew the total amount of salt dissolved in the ocean and the amount added each year, it might be possible to calculate the ocean's age. In 1899, Irish geologist John Joly (1857–1933) attempted the calculation. From information provided by gauges placed at the mouths of streams, Joly was able to estimate the annual increment of salt to the oceans. Then, knowing the salinity of ocean water and the approximate volume of water, he calculated the amount of salt already held in solution in the oceans. An estimate of the age of the ocean was obtained by dividing the total salt in the ocean by the rate of salt added each year. Beginning with essentially non-saline oceans, it would have taken about 90 million years for the oceans to reach their present salinity, according to Joly. The figure, however, was off the currently accepted mark of 4.54 billion by a factor of 50, largely because there was no way to account accurately for recycled salt and salt incorporated into clay minerals deposited on the sea floors. Even though in error, Joly's calculations clearly supported those geologists who insisted on an age for Earth far in excess of a few million years. The belief in Earth's immense antiquity was also supported by Darwin, Huxley, and other evolutionary biologists, who saw the need for time in the hundreds of millions of years to accomplish the organic evolution apparent in the fossil record.

另一種估算地球年齡的方法，是 1715 年由 Edmund Halley 爵士（1656–1742）所提出的——我們最常把他的名字與那顆著名的彗星聯想在一起。Halley 推測：海洋是在地球誕生後不久就形成的，因此年齡只會比固態地球稍微年輕一點。他推論：原始的海洋並不是鹹的，後來才慢慢由岩石風化所形成的鹽，藉由河流匯入海洋。因此，如果你知道海洋中總共溶有多少鹽，以及每年新增多少鹽，就可以計算出海洋的年齡。1899 年，愛爾蘭地質學家 John Joly（1857–1933）嘗試了這項計算。透過設置在河口的量測儀器資料，Joly 能估算每年進入海洋的鹽量。接著，知道海水的鹽度與海水的大致總量，他算出海洋中已溶有的總鹽量。將「海洋總鹽量」除以「每年新增鹽量」，就能得到海洋年齡的估算值。Joly 計算結果顯示：如果一開始海洋幾乎不含鹽，則達到目前的鹽度大約需要 9,000 萬年。不過，這個數字與如今公認的 45.4 億年相差了 50 倍——主要原因是當時無法準確計入「再循環的鹽」以及被沉積在海底黏土礦物中的鹽。儘管有誤，Joly 的計算明顯支持了那些主張地球年齡遠遠超過數百萬年的地質學家。相信地球極為古老的觀點，也獲得達爾文、赫胥黎以及其他演化生物學家的支持——他們認為，要完成化石紀錄中明顯可見的有機演化，需要數億年的時間。
