## Overkill of the North American Megafauna

---

### Paragraph 1

Thousands of years ago, in North America's past, all of its megafauna—large mammals such as mammoths and giant bears—disappeared. One proposed explanation for this event is that when the first Americans migrated over from Asia, they hunted the megafauna to extinction. These people, known as the Clovis society after a site where their distinctive spear points were first found, would have been able to use this food source to expand their population and fill the continent rapidly. Yet many scientists argue against this "Pleistocene overkill" hypothesis. Modern humans have certainly been capable of such drastic effects on animals, but could ancient people with little more than stone spears similarly have caused the extinction of numerous species of animals? Thirty-five genera or groups of species (and many individual species) suffered extinction in North America around 11,000 B.C., soon after the appearance and expansion of Paleo-Indians throughout the Americas (27 genera disappeared completely, and another 8 became locally extinct, surviving only outside North America).

<summary>翻譯 Translation</summary>

數千年前，北美洲所有的「大型動物群（megafauna）」——例如猛瑪象和巨熊這樣的大型哺乳動物——全部消失了。一種被提出的解釋是：當最早的美洲人從亞洲遷徙過來時，他們把這些大型動物獵殺到滅絕。這群人被稱為「克洛維斯社會（Clovis society）」，這個名字取自他們獨特的矛頭最早被發現的地點；他們可能利用這個食物來源讓人口擴張，並迅速遍布整個大陸。然而許多科學家反對這個「更新世過度獵殺（Pleistocene overkill）」假說。現代人類確實有能力對動物造成如此劇烈的影響，但僅僅靠著石矛的古代人類，真的也能讓那麼多動物物種滅絕嗎？大約在西元前 11,000 年，北美洲有 35 個屬（或族群）以及許多個別物種滅絕，剛好就在古印第安人出現並擴散到整個美洲之後不久（其中 27 個屬完全消失，另外 8 個在當地滅絕，只在北美以外的地方倖存）。

### Paragraph 7

Though Martin claims the lack of evidence actually supports his model—the evidence is sparse because the spread of humans and the extinction of animals occurred so quickly—this argument seems weak. And how could we ever disprove it? As archaeologist Donald Grayson points out, in other cases where extinction resulted from the quick spread of human hunters—for example, the extinction of the moa, the large flightless bird of New Zealand—archaeological evidence in the form of remains is abundant. Grayson has also shown that the evidence is not so clear that all or even most of the large herbivores in late Pleistocene America became extinct after the appearance of Clovis. Of the 35 extinct genera, only 8 can be confidently assigned an extinction date of between 12,000 and 10,000 years ago. Many of the older genera, Grayson argues, may have succumbed before 12,000 B.C., at least half a century before the Clovis showed up in the American West.

<details>
<summary>翻譯 Translation</summary>

雖然 Martin 主張「證據缺乏反而支持他的模型——因為人類擴散與動物滅絕都發生得太快，所以證據才會稀少」，但這種論述顯得薄弱。而且我們又要如何反駁這種說法呢？正如考古學家 Donald Grayson 所指出的，在其他「因人類獵人快速擴散而導致滅絕」的案例中——例如紐西蘭那種大型不會飛的鳥「moa」的滅絕——以遺骸形式留下的考古證據是非常豐富的。Grayson 也指出，「更新世晚期美洲所有、甚至大多數大型草食動物都是在克洛維斯出現之後才滅絕」這個說法，證據其實沒那麼明確。在這 35 個已滅絕的屬中，只有 8 個能有信心地把滅絕時間定在 12,000 到 10,000 年前之間。Grayson 主張，許多較古老的屬可能早在西元前 12,000 年之前就已經消亡——也就是至少在克洛維斯人出現於美國西部的半個世紀之前。

</details>
