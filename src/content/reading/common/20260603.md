Hi, I'm Blair Allsopp. I'm a Brisbane `greeter`, and it's my pleasure to show people around Brisbane. The cool thing about Brisbane is it's a great city for cycling. There's a thousand kilometers of `off-track` cycling for people to get around the city. It's nice and `flat`. It's easy to go.

We have a share bike system— two dollars a day. You can hire a bike and ride around to your heart's content. It's a great way to see.
Here we are on the Goodwill Bridge. This bridge is a `pedestrian`-and-bicycle-only bridge —no cars to worry about. And the great thing about this bridge-there's a coffee `cart` right in the middle of it.
The cool thing about the café on the Goodwill Bridge and Brendan—he's down here every morning at five o'clock making coffees, getting ready for the cyclists and runners coming through. And the beauty about that is, on his Facebook page when he sets up in the morning, he posts a picture of what the day is like. So, at home before you start your ride or your run, you can go to your Facebook page, check out Brendan's photo and think, "It's a good day for a ride.”

---

One of the great `legacies` of Expo 88 is this `fantastic` water park. We have this huge `lagoon` with a beach. It's called Streets Beach. This is the size of five Olympic swimming pools of totally free, treated water. And here we are an hour away from the coast, yet we have our own beach right here in the middle of the city. How great's that?
So, we're here in Brisbane Square. This is where the markets are on Wednesday. It's great to be here in the city that you can come down from your  office building, and come down and have lunch here with all this fresh fruit and vegetables. It's just a fantastic environment. It's a really cool square too, 'cause it's got the old buildings, the new buildings. We have office workers. We have `organic` food grown from the farmers. So, it's sort of the country in the city, all brought together.
Here we are, some Queensland nuts, or to some people known around the world as `macadamias`, but they're actually originally from Queensland.

---

Well, right across from the farmers market here in George Street, we have Brisbane's most `photographed` piece of public art—our farm `machinery` kangaroos. And behind me, Burnett Lane, the oldest laneway in Brisbane. Come on. Let's have a look.
And down at the end of Burnett Lane is one of my favorite coffee shops-Brew. It's down here in the basement. Brew, Brisbane's first `laneway` coffee shop.
During the daytime, you come down here, they'll brew you a cup of coffee. If you come down at night, it turns into a live music and beer and wine bar.

So, you can get a different kind of brew. It's after four o'clock. I think I'll have a beer.
So, at the end of the day, it's really nice to be able to take a Brisbane CityCat(布里斯本的渡船) on your way home. So, we use the river here as a way of public `transport`. It's a great way to get along the river. Now, it's really a nice way to `commute`, at the end of a day to get out on the water, get a nice fresh `breeze`, particularly if it's a hot summer's day. Really nice at night if you're going out to a show at the end of a night, and you can have dinner. And you get on the boat and you get this beautiful trip home where you can get all the lights of the city on your way home. And you really `embrace` Brisbane as a river city.
