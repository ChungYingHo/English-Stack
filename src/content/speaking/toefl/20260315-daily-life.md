## Task 2 — Interview Practice

### Set 1: Sleep and Daily Routines

You have volunteered for a research study about sleep habits and daily routines. You will have a short online interview with a researcher. The researcher will ask you some questions.

---

#### Q1 — Personal Experience

Thank you for taking part in this study. Today, I'd like to ask you some questions about your sleep habits and daily routines. First, **do you consider yourself a morning person who wakes up early, or a night person who stays up late? Why?**

---

#### Q2 — Preference / Hypothetical Choice

I see. Imagine you have a busy week ahead. **You can sleep fewer hours each night to have more time for work and activities, or keep a full eight hours of sleep but have less free time. Which would you choose, and why?**

---

#### Q3 — Agree or Disagree

Interesting. Next, I'd like to get your opinion. **Some people believe that schools and workplaces should start later in the morning to allow people to get more sleep. Do you agree or disagree? Why or why not?**

---

#### Q4 — Positive and Negative Effects

Good points. Lastly, wearable devices that track sleep patterns and suggest bedtime routines are becoming more common. **How could widespread use of sleep-tracking technology affect people's daily lives in positive and negative ways? Explain your answer.**

### Set 2: Language Learning

You have agreed to participate in a research study about language learning. You will have a short online interview with a researcher. The researcher will ask you some questions.

---

#### Q1 — Personal Experience

Thank you for joining the study. Today, I'd like to ask you some questions about language learning. First, **have you or someone you know ever tried to learn a foreign language? What method did you or they use, such as classes, apps, or conversation practice?**

---

#### Q2 — Preference / Hypothetical Choice

I see. Imagine you want to learn a new language. **You can take a structured course with a teacher, which provides guidance but follows a fixed schedule, or use a self-paced app, which is flexible but requires strong motivation. Which would you choose, and why?**

---

#### Q3 — Agree or Disagree

Interesting. Next, I'd like to get your opinion. **Some people believe that the best way to learn a language is to live in a country where it is spoken, rather than studying it in a classroom. Do you agree or disagree? Why or why not?**

---

#### Q4 — Positive and Negative Effects

Good points. Lastly, AI translation tools are becoming increasingly accurate, allowing people to communicate across languages instantly. **How could the widespread use of real-time translation technology affect language learning in positive and negative ways? Explain your answer.**

### Set 4: Pets and Animal Care

You have agreed to participate in a research study about pets and animal care. You will have a short online interview with a researcher. The researcher will ask you some questions.

---

#### Q1 — Personal Experience

Thank you for participating today. I'd like to ask you some questions about pets and animal care. First, **do you or does anyone in your family currently have a pet? If so, what kind of pet, and if not, would you like to have one? Why?**

---

#### Q2 — Preference / Hypothetical Choice

I see. Imagine you decide to get a pet. **You can adopt a rescue animal from a shelter, which may have unknown health history but needs a home, or buy a specific breed from a breeder, which is predictable but more expensive. Which would you choose, and why?**

---

#### Q3 — Agree or Disagree

Interesting. Next, I'd like to get your opinion. **Some people believe that keeping pets in small apartments is unfair to the animals. Do you agree or disagree? Why or why not?**

---

#### Q4 — Positive and Negative Effects

Good points. Lastly, some cities are creating more pet-friendly public spaces, such as off-leash parks and pet-friendly cafes and offices. **How could making cities more pet-friendly affect residents in positive and negative ways? Explain your answer.**
