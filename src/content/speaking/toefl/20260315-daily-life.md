## Task 2 — Interview Practice

### Set 1: Sleep and Daily Routines

You have volunteered for a research study about sleep habits and daily routines. You will have a short online interview with a researcher. The researcher will ask you some questions.

---

#### Q1 — Personal Experience

Thank you for taking part in this study. Today, I'd like to ask you some questions about your sleep habits and daily routines. First, **do you consider yourself a morning person who wakes up early, or a night person who stays up late? Why?**

**公式答（時間／金錢／人）**

I prefer being a morning person very much. First of all, I feel fresh and focused in the morning. My brain is clear and I can finish my homework really fast. Also, I can study quietly before my classes start. The house is silent and no friends are sending me phone messages yet. Finally, I sleep early every night and feel healthy every day. I never feel tired in class because I always get a full eight hours. I also have more energy to play sports with my family after school. That is why I really think being a morning person is the best choice.

---

#### Q2 — Preference / Hypothetical Choice

I see. Imagine you have a busy week ahead. **You can sleep fewer hours each night to have more time for work and activities, or keep a full eight hours of sleep but have less free time. Which would you choose, and why?**

**公式答（時間／金錢／人）**

I really prefer a full eight hours of sleep every night. It saves time because I can finish my work really fast the next day. My brain works well and I do not make silly mistakes at school. It also saves money because I do not need coffee to stay awake. My wallet is happy and my stomach feels better every morning. Many people in Taiwan also choose this way. My parents and friends all try to sleep eight hours every night too. So overall, I really think a full night of sleep is the best choice.

---

#### Q3 — Agree or Disagree

Interesting. Next, I'd like to get your opinion. **Some people believe that schools and workplaces should start later in the morning to allow people to get more sleep. Do you agree or disagree? Why or why not?**

**公式答（時間／金錢／人）**

I agree with this idea very much. First of all, students can sleep more and really focus in class. They are not yawning or falling asleep during the first lesson of the day. Also, workers feel much less tired in the morning on the way to work. They drive more safely and make fewer mistakes at their jobs during the day. Finally, families have more time to eat a nice breakfast together. Parents and kids can chat and plan the day before school starts. That is why I think schools and workplaces should start a bit later.

---

#### Q4 — Positive and Negative Effects

Good points. Lastly, wearable devices that track sleep patterns and suggest bedtime routines are becoming more common. **How could widespread use of sleep-tracking technology affect people's daily lives in positive and negative ways? Explain your answer.**

**公式答（時間／金錢／人）**

I think sleep-tracking technology has many good effects. It saves time because I just use my phone and check my sleep fast. I do not need to write anything down or count the hours I slept. It also saves money because I do not need to see a doctor first. The app gives me simple tips for free every single morning. Many people in Taiwan also use this method every day. My friends love their smart watches and share sleep scores in our chat. So overall, I really think sleep apps are a good tool for everyone.
