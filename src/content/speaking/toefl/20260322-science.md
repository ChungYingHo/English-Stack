I really think spending time with grandparents is very important. First of all, they teach us family stories and old traditions. I learn about my family history and old festivals from their stories. Finally, grandparents feel loved when young people visit them on weekends. My grandmother always smiles really big when I walk into her small apartment. I see. In many families, caring for aging relatives involves different approaches. Some families keep elderly members at home, while others seek professional care facilities. **What is the typical approach in your family or community, and what do you think influences that choice?**

**公式答（時間／金錢／人）**

I prefer keeping elderly relatives at home very much. It saves time because families do not need to drive far to visit them. My mom can check on grandpa during lunch break and still work. It also saves money because care homes are really not cheap in Taiwan. Most families would need to pay a big bill every single month. Many people in Taiwan also choose this way for cultural reasons. My neighbors and friends all take care of their grandparents at home together. So overall, I really think keeping elderly family at home is the best way.

If a senior has a sudden fall or a medication error at home, it can be fatal, but in a facility, they have immediate access to emergency response equipment. Furthermore, this transition changes the dynamic of family relationships. Instead of spending every visit performing stressful physical labor like bathing or feeding, children can actually enjoy quality social time with their parents. This prevents the resentment that often builds up when a family member is forced into a full-time nursing role and allows for a much healthier emotional connection.
