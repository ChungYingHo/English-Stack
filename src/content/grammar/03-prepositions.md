## to vs for

這兩個最常混，但邏輯很清楚：

### to — 方向、目標、對象

- give something **to** someone — 給某人（動作方向）
- go **to** the store
- write **to** him
- He explained the concept **to** the students.
- The findings were presented **to** the committee.
- The grant was awarded **to** the research team.

### for — 目的、利益、代替

- do something **for** someone — 為了某人的利益
- buy a gift **for** her
- used **for** measuring temperature — 用來做某事的目的
- This instrument is designed **for** detecting trace amounts of CO₂.
- The study was conducted **for** the purpose of identifying risk factors.

分辨訣竅：

- 「**接收動作的人**」→ to（She sent a letter **to** him.）
- 「**受益的人 / 目的**」→ for（She bought a gift **for** him.）

## in / on / at（時間）

由大到小：`in` → `on` → `at`

- `in` — 較長的時間段：in 2024, in March, in the morning, in the 20th century, in the 1990s, in the Ice Age
- `on` — 特定某一天：on Monday, on March 14th, on the weekend, on New Year's Eve, on Christmas Day
- `at` — 精確時間點：at 9 a.m., at noon, at midnight, at the moment, at dawn, at dusk, at that time

特別注意：

- **in the morning / afternoon / evening**（but **at night**）
- **in the 1990s**（用 in 搭配年代）
- **at the turn of the century**（固定說法）
- **on the morning of March 5th**（特定某天的早上，用 on）

:::tip
記法：越精確，介系詞越「小」。年份/月份/年代 → in；特定某天 → on；精確時刻 → at。
:::

---

## in / on / at（地點）

- `in` — 在某個空間「裡面」：in the room, in New York, in the ocean, in the forest, in the Northern Hemisphere
- `on` — 在某個面「上面」：on the table, on the wall, on the street, on the page, on the coast, on the surface
- `at` — 在某個「點」或「場所功能」：at the door, at school, at the airport, at home, at the intersection

特別注意：
- live **in** New York（城市用 in）
- arrive **in** New York / arrive **at** the airport（大地方用 in，小地方/設施用 at）
- **on** the island / **on** the peninsula（島嶼半島表面，用 on）
- **in** the ocean / **in** the lake（水體內部，用 in）
- **at** the top of / **at** the bottom of（位置上的點，用 at）

## during vs for vs while

這三個都和「時間」有關，但後面接的東西完全不同，不能互換。

| | 後面接什麼 | 意思 |
|---|---|---|
| during | 名詞 / 名詞片語 | 在某個時期「期間」|
| for | 時間長度 | 持續「多長時間」|
| while | 子句（有主詞+動詞）| 「當…的時候」|

- The ice expanded **during** the winter months.（during + 名詞）
- The ice expanded **for** three months.（for + 時間長度）
- The ice expanded **while** temperatures remained low.（while + 子句）
- Migration rates increased **during** the Industrial Revolution.
- Migration rates increased **for** over a century.
- Migration rates increased **while** economic conditions remained unstable.

常見錯誤：

- ✗ During the experiment was conducted, they observed...（during 後不接子句）
- ✓ While the experiment was conducted, they observed...
- ✗ For the summer, temperatures rise significantly.（不是「持續多長」，而是「在某段期間」）
- ✓ During the summer, temperatures rise significantly.

:::tip
口訣：during + 名詞（a period / the war / summer）；for + 數字+時間單位（three months / a decade）；while + 主詞 + 動詞（子句）。
:::

## within vs by

- **within** = 在某個時間**範圍之內**（不超過某段時間）
  - Results will be available **within** two weeks.（兩週之內的某個時間）
  - The temperature must stay **within** acceptable limits.
  - The project was completed **within** the allocated budget.
  - **Within** a few decades, the region was transformed.

- **by** = 不超過某個**截止時間點**
  - Please submit your report **by** Friday.（最晚到星期五）
  - The project must be completed **by** the end of the year.
  - All data must be verified **by** the time the paper is submitted.
  - **By** 2050, sea levels are projected to rise significantly.

常見錯誤：

- ✗ Complete it **within** Friday.（within 接時間段，不接特定日期點）
- ✓ Complete it **by** Friday.
- ✗ Submit the report **by** three days.（by 接特定時間點，不接時間長度）
- ✓ Submit the report **within** three days.

## of / about / with / by

### of — 所屬、組成

- the results **of** the study
- a lack **of** evidence
- the majority **of** participants
- the rate **of** change
- the absence **of** significant variation

### about — 主題、關於

- a book **about** climate change
- concerned **about** the results
- There is growing debate **about** the long-term implications.

### with — 伴隨、工具、特徵

- mix A **with** B
- a person **with** experience
- **With** limited resources, they succeeded.
- consistent **with** the hypothesis
- associated **with** higher mortality rates

### by — 方式、執行者（被動）、期限

- discovered **by** scientists（被動語態的執行者）
- travel **by** train（交通方式）
- finish **by** Friday（不超過某個時間點）
- measured **by** satellite（方式）
- divided **by** two（數學運算）

## across / through / along / over / under / throughout

### across — 橫越某個表面（from one side to the other）

- The study was conducted **across** twelve countries.
- Signals travel **across** the network with minimal latency.
- The concept spread **across** multiple disciplines.
- **Across** the region, similar patterns were observed.

### through — 穿越某個空間內部

- Water moves **through** the soil layers via capillary action.
- The concept spread **through** the scientific community over several decades.
- Light passes **through** the atmosphere and is partially absorbed.
- **Through** careful analysis, the team identified the source of error.

### along — 沿著某條線、路線

- Settlements developed **along** the river, taking advantage of fertile floodplains.
- **Along** the coast, erosion has been accelerating due to rising sea levels.
- Trade routes were established **along** mountain passes.

### over — 在…上方；超過某個數量；在某段期間

- **Over** the past decade, global temperatures have risen by 0.2°C.
- The bridge extends **over** the valley, spanning 400 meters.
- A study involving **over** 500 participants found no significant correlation.
- The debate **over** this issue has continued for years.

### throughout — 在整個時間或空間範圍內（from beginning to end / from one end to the other）

- **Throughout** the 20th century, urbanization accelerated dramatically.
- The pattern was observed **throughout** the region, with no exceptions.
- **Throughout** the study period, temperature remained stable.
- The species is distributed **throughout** the tropical rainforest belt.

### under — 在…下方；少於某數量；在某個條件/狀態下

- **Under** normal conditions, the reaction occurs quickly and completely.
- **Under** pressure, the material deforms and eventually fractures.
- The temperature remained **under** 0°C for the entire winter.
- The research was conducted **under** strict ethical guidelines.
- Children **under** the age of twelve were excluded from the study.

## between vs among

- `between` — 兩者之間，或明確列舉的個體之間

  - the difference **between** A and B
  - negotiations **between** the three countries（明確個體）
  - the relationship **between** cause and effect
  - **Between** 1990 and 2020, the global average temperature rose by 0.6°C.

- `among` — 三者以上的群體之中（模糊、集體）

  - popular **among** young people
  - **among** the various theories proposed
  - The practice is widespread **among** hunter-gatherer societies.
  - **Among** the most significant findings was the discovery of...

## 形容詞 + 介系詞固定搭配（Adjective + Preposition Collocations）

這些是固定搭配，不能換介系詞：

- aware **of** — The scientists were **aware of** the limitations of their methodology.
- responsible **for** — The government is **responsible for** regulating emissions.
- capable **of** — The method is **capable of** detecting minute changes in concentration.
- interested **in** — Researchers are **interested in** the long-term effects of exposure.
- concerned **about** — Scientists are **concerned about** rising ocean temperatures.
- associated **with** — The disease is **associated with** poor sanitation and overcrowding.
- dependent **on** / independent **of** — The result is **dependent on** the ambient temperature. The two variables are **independent of** each other.
- similar **to** / different **from** — This species is **similar to** its relative but **different from** the common ancestor.
- familiar **with** — Readers **familiar with** the theory will recognize the implications.
- characteristic **of** — This layered structure is **characteristic of** sedimentary rock.
- consistent **with** — The findings are **consistent with** the original hypothesis.
- relevant **to** — The data are **relevant to** our understanding of ocean circulation.
- based **on** — The model is **based on** three fundamental assumptions.
- limited **to** — The study was **limited to** urban populations in high-income countries.
- composed **of** — The team is **composed of** twelve researchers from six institutions.
- divided **into** — The experiment was **divided into** three distinct phases.
- exposed **to** — Participants were **exposed to** varying levels of ultraviolet light.
- involved **in** — Several international institutions were **involved in** the project.
- superior **to** / inferior **to** — This method is **superior to** conventional approaches in terms of precision.
- prior **to** — **Prior to** the experiment, all equipment was carefully calibrated.（= before）
- subject **to** — All results are **subject to** peer review before publication.
- specific **to** — This adaptation is **specific to** high-altitude environments.
- central **to** — This concept is **central to** the entire argument.

:::note
形容詞+介系詞搭配沒有邏輯規律，只能記憶。遇到不確定的，先想「這個形容詞的常見固定搭配是什麼」，而不是猜「哪個介系詞感覺對」。
:::

## 常見動詞 + 介系詞固定搭配

這些組合是固定的，不能換介系詞：

- agree **with** someone / agree **on** a topic / agree **to** a plan
- account **for**（佔…比例 / 解釋原因）
- associate **with**（與…相關）
- compare A **with/to** B（compare **with** = 拿A和B相比；compare **to** = 把A比作B）
- comply **with**（遵守）
- consist **of**（由…組成）
- contribute **to**（有助於 / 貢獻）
- contrast **with**（與…對比）
- deal **with**（處理）
- depend **on**（取決於）
- derive **from**（源自）
- differ **from**（與…不同）
- focus **on**（專注於）
- interfere **with**（干擾）
- invest **in**（投資）
- lead **to**（導向）
- object **to**（反對）
- participate **in**（參與）
- proceed **with**（繼續進行）
- refer **to**（指的是）
- rely **on**（依賴）
- respond **to**（回應）
- result **in**（導致某結果）
- result **from**（起因於某原因）
- stem **from**（起源於）
- subscribe **to**（支持某觀點）

特別注意 `result in` vs `result from`：

- Pollution **results from** industrial activity.（起因：industrial activity 是原因）
- Industrial activity **results in** pollution.（結果：pollution 是後果）
- Deforestation **results from** agricultural expansion.
- Agricultural expansion **results in** habitat loss.

:::tip
口訣：result **from** = 起因（← 往左看原因）；result **in** = 結果（→ 往右看後果）。可以用 because of / lead to 互換來確認：results from X ≈ is caused by X；results in Y ≈ leads to Y。
:::

> 遇到不確定的介系詞，不要猜「哪個感覺對」，先問自己「這個動詞的固定搭配是什麼」。
