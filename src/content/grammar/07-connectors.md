## 為什麼連接詞很重要

TOEFL 閱讀中，理解「句子之間的邏輯關係」是答題關鍵。連接詞就是作者給你的邏輯信號，告訴你接下來是「補充」還是「反駁」還是「因果」。

連接詞分三大類，用法不同：

- **連接詞（Conjunction）**：連接兩個子句，中間不加分號。如：*although, because, while*
- **副詞連接詞（Conjunctive Adverb）**：放在新句子開頭（或中間），前面通常加分號或句號。如：*however, therefore, moreover*
- **介系詞片語**：後面接名詞或動名詞，不接子句。如：*despite, because of, due to*

---

## 正式 vs 非正式

寫 TOEFL 作文時，選對語域非常重要。以下整理哪些連接詞更適合學術寫作，哪些偏口語應避免。

### 較正式（TOEFL 寫作首選）

- **furthermore / moreover / in addition** — 遞進補充
- **however / nevertheless / nonetheless** — 轉折讓步
- **consequently / therefore / thus / hence** — 因果結論
- **in contrast / by contrast** — 對比
- **notwithstanding** — 儘管（尤其正式，可代替 despite）
- **given that / provided that** — 條件
- **whereas / while** — 對比（學術文章常見）

### 較口語（TOEFL 寫作應避免放句首）

- **also**（放句首當連接詞使用）→ 改用 furthermore / in addition
- **plus**（用作連接詞）→ 改用 moreover / additionally
- **so**（作連接詞開頭段落）→ 改用 therefore / consequently
- **but**（放段落句首）→ 改用 however / nevertheless

## 對比 & 讓步（儘管 / 但是）

### 連接詞（接子句）

- **although / even though / though** — 儘管…（強調後面的主句）
  - **Although** the sample was small, the results were statistically significant.
  - **Even though** the experiment was replicated five times, results remained inconsistent.
  - The method was unconventional, **though** it proved highly effective.

- **while / whereas** — 兩者對比（A 是這樣，B 是那樣）
  - Mammals are warm-blooded, **while** reptiles are cold-blooded.
  - **Whereas** earlier models relied on manual input, the new system is fully automated.
  - Some species thrived in the altered environment, **while** others declined rapidly.

### although vs even though vs though（程度差異）

- **even though** 強調讓步更強（即使如此仍然…），語氣最強
- **although** 最正式，最常見於學術文章
- **though** 較口語，也可放句尾（非正式：*It was difficult, though.*）

- ✓ **Although** the evidence was limited, the findings were considered significant.
- ✓ **Even though** the experiment was repeated multiple times, the anomaly persisted.

### 副詞連接詞（放新句子開頭）

- **however** — 然而，轉折
  - The theory was widely accepted. **However**, new evidence challenges it.
  - The study produced promising results. **However**, the sample size was too small to generalize.

- **nevertheless / nonetheless** — 儘管如此（讓步後仍堅持）
  - The conditions were harsh. **Nevertheless**, the team completed the study on schedule.
  - Funding was scarce. **Nonetheless**, the researchers produced internationally recognized work.

- **on the other hand** — 另一方面
  - Solar energy is clean. **On the other hand**, it requires significant infrastructure investment.
  - The drug showed clear benefits. **On the other hand**, the side effects were concerning.

- **in contrast / by contrast** — 相比之下
  - Developed nations emit more CO₂. **In contrast**, developing nations face more severe climate impacts.
  - Early studies showed positive results. **By contrast**, more recent trials have been inconclusive.

### however vs but（重要區別）

- **but** 直接連接兩個子句，中間加逗號，不加分號
- **however** 放在新句子或子句開頭，前面要用句號或分號

- ✓ The theory was compelling, **but** the evidence was thin.
- ✓ The theory was compelling. **However**, the evidence was thin.
- ✓ The theory was compelling; **however**, the evidence was thin.（分號可以）
- ✗ The theory was compelling, **however**, the evidence was thin.（逗號不夠）

:::tip
口訣：`however` 前面必須是句號或分號，不能只用逗號。`but` 前面可以只用逗號。這是 TOEFL 寫作常考的標點錯誤！
:::

### 介系詞片語（後接名詞/動名詞）

- **despite / in spite of** — 儘管（後接名詞或動名詞，不接子句）
  - **Despite** the limited funding, the research was successfully completed.
  - **In spite of** numerous setbacks, the team persisted with their original methodology.
  - ✗ Despite **they had** limited funding...（despite 後不能接子句）
  - ✓ Despite **having** limited funding...（接動名詞可以）
  - ✓ Despite **the limited** funding...（接名詞片語）

## 因果關係

### 表「原因」（因為）

- **because / since / as**（連接詞，接子句）
  - **Because** the sample size was too small, the results were inconclusive.
  - **Since** no control group was used, the findings are difficult to interpret.
  - **As** demand for the resource increased, prices rose sharply.

- **because of / due to / owing to**（介系詞片語，接名詞）
  - The experiment was delayed **due to** equipment failure.
  - The project was suspended **because of** a lack of funding.
  - **Owing to** the extreme weather conditions, data collection was interrupted.
  - ✗ Due to **the equipment failed**...（後面不接子句）
  - ✓ Due to **equipment failure**...（接名詞片語）

### 表「結果」（因此）

- **therefore / thus / hence / as a result / consequently**
  - The data were incomplete. **Therefore**, no definitive conclusions could be drawn.
  - Resources were limited; **thus**, the team had to prioritize the most critical variables.
  - The sample was contaminated; **hence**, the entire batch had to be discarded.
  - The new treatment showed no benefit. **As a result**, the trial was terminated early.
  - The population declined sharply over two decades. **Consequently**, the species was listed as endangered.

- **so**（連接詞，較口語）
  - The sample was contaminated, **so** the experiment was repeated from the beginning.

### result in vs result from（重溫）

- Industrial emissions **result in** air pollution.（A 導致 B → result in）
- Air pollution **results from** industrial emissions.（B 起因於 A → result from）
- Habitat loss **results in** a decline in biodiversity.
- A decline in biodiversity often **results from** multiple interacting pressures.

## 遞進 & 補充（而且、此外）

- **furthermore / moreover / in addition / additionally**
  - The method is cost-effective. **Furthermore**, it is environmentally friendly.
  - The study was thorough. **Moreover**, it was successfully replicated in three countries.
  - The treatment reduced pain significantly. **In addition**, patients reported improved mobility.
  - The algorithm is faster. **Additionally**, it requires far less computational power.

- **also / besides / as well as**
  - The treatment reduced pain. It **also** improved mobility and sleep quality.
  - **Besides** reducing cost, the new method also improves measurement accuracy.
  - The study examined behavioral changes **as well as** physiological responses.

## 時間連接詞

### before / after — 時間先後

- **After** the data were verified, the team published their findings in a peer-reviewed journal.
- **Before** drawing conclusions, researchers must rule out all plausible confounding factors.
- **After** conducting a thorough review, the committee approved the revised protocol.

### once / as soon as — 一旦…就…

- **Once** the temperature drops below 0°C, the chemical reaction stops entirely.
- **As soon as** the results were confirmed, the team submitted the paper for review.
- **Once** the new equipment arrived, the experiment could be resumed.

### until / by the time — 直到 / 等到

- Researchers continued testing **until** a consistent and reproducible pattern emerged.
- **By the time** the rescue team arrived, the survivors had already left the area.
- The data were not fully processed **until** three weeks after collection.

### as / when — 當…時（表伴隨或同時）

- **As** industrialization accelerated, pollution levels rose sharply across the region.（表伴隨過程）
- **When** the funding was cut, the project had to be significantly scaled back.（表特定時間點）
- **As** the population aged, demand for healthcare services increased proportionally.

## 條件連接詞

- **if** — 一般條件
  - **If** the temperature rises by 2°C, coastal flooding will become more frequent.

- **unless** — 除非（= if not）
  - **Unless** adequate measures are taken, the situation will continue to worsen.
  - ✓ **Unless** the sample is properly stored, degradation will occur.（= If the sample is not properly stored...）

- **provided (that) / providing (that)** — 前提是，只要
  - The study can proceed **provided that** ethical approval is granted by the review board.
  - The method is valid **providing that** all variables are carefully controlled.

- **as long as** — 只要（條件持續成立）
  - The method is effective **as long as** the sample size is sufficient.
  - The collaboration will continue **as long as** both institutions contribute equally.

- **in case** — 以防（萬一）
  - Back up the data regularly **in case** the system fails or is compromised.
  - The team brought extra equipment **in case** any instruments malfunctioned in the field.

## 讓步後強調（雖然…但…）

- **admittedly** — 不可否認地（承認對方的觀點）
  - **Admittedly**, the sample size was small, but the effect size was substantial.
  - **Admittedly**, the methodology has limitations, yet the findings remain noteworthy.

- **it is true that...but** — 確實…但…
  - **It is true that** the experiment has limitations, **but** the core findings are still significant.
  - **It is true that** early results were mixed, **but** subsequent trials confirmed the effect.

## 舉例 & 說明

- **for example / for instance**
  - Many animals migrate seasonally. **For example**, Arctic terns travel from pole to pole each year.
  - Some ecosystems are especially vulnerable. **For instance**, coral reefs can be destroyed by a rise of just 1°C.

- **such as / including**（後接名詞，不用完整子句）
  - Marine mammals, **such as** dolphins and whales, rely heavily on echolocation for navigation.
  - Several factors, **including** temperature, salinity, and pH, affect marine biodiversity.

- **specifically / in particular**
  - Climate change affects biodiversity. **In particular**, coral reefs are under serious threat.
  - The study focused on tropical ecosystems, **specifically** those found in Southeast Asia.

- **that is / i.e. / namely**（重新定義或說明）
  - The study used a double-blind method — **that is**, neither the participants nor the researchers knew who received the treatment.
  - Only one variable was manipulated — **namely**, the concentration of the active compound.

---

## 總結 & 結論

- **in conclusion / in summary / to summarize**
  - **In conclusion**, the evidence strongly supports the proposed hypothesis.
  - **To summarize**, three key factors were found to influence the outcome.

- **overall / in general**
  - **Overall**, the results suggest a positive correlation between the two variables.
  - **In general**, studies conducted over longer time periods tend to yield more reliable data.

## 快速辨認邏輯關係

閱讀時看到這些詞，立刻判斷作者的意圖：

- **however / but / yet** → 前後矛盾，準備反駁
- **therefore / thus / hence** → 前面是原因，這裡是結論
- **although / even though** → 讓步，但主句才是重點
- **furthermore / moreover** → 繼續加碼，方向一致
- **for example / such as** → 舉例，是補充說明不是新論點
- **in contrast / whereas** → 兩個東西做比較
- **once / as soon as** → 有前提條件，時間先後
- **unless / provided that** → 有條件限制
