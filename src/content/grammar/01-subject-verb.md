## 第三人稱單數

主詞是 **he / she / it**，或任何「單數名詞」時，動詞加 `-s`。

- ✓ The report **suggests** that demand is rising.
- ✓ The government **has** released new guidelines.
- ✓ The hypothesis **requires** further testing before it can be accepted.
- ✓ Each experiment **demonstrates** a consistent pattern across all trials.
- ✓ The evidence **points** to a strong correlation between the two variables.
- ✗ The report suggest... / The government have...

否定跟疑問句用 `does`，動詞回原形：

- She **doesn't** agree. / **Does** the data show anything?
- The model **doesn't** account for seasonal variation.
- **Does** the study **support** the original hypothesis?

:::tip
口訣：任何單數名詞都等於 it。不管名詞多長，找到主詞，問自己「這等於 he/she/it 還是 they？」
:::

## 主詞動詞一致

### 中間插了修飾語，不影響動詞

動詞要看**最前面的主詞**，不要被夾在中間的片語帶跑。這是最常見的考點。

- The **quality** of the samples *collected from three different sites* **is** high.
  （主詞是 quality，不是 samples 或 sites）

- The **scientists** who conducted the five-year study on endangered species **were** from MIT.
  （主詞是 scientists，不是 study 或 species）

- The **impact** of rising sea levels on coastal ecosystems **has** been well documented.
  （主詞是 impact，不是 levels 或 ecosystems）

- The **rate** at which glaciers in the Arctic region are retreating **has** accelerated dramatically.
  （主詞是 rate，不是 glaciers 或 region）

- A **theory** supported by multiple independent lines of evidence **is** more likely to be accepted.
  （主詞是 theory，不是 lines 或 evidence）

:::note
TOEFL 閱讀裡的長主詞結構往往是：**核心名詞 + of/who/that/which + 修飾語**。找主詞的方法：從句首開始，遇到第一個動詞，往前找距它最近且沒有被介系詞「綁走」的名詞。
:::

### 複合主詞

`A and B` → 動詞**複數**：

- Water and sunlight **are** essential for plant growth.
- Temperature and humidity **affect** the rate of chemical reactions.
- The researchers and their advisors **have** reviewed the findings.

`A or B` / `Either A or B` / `Neither A nor B` → 動詞看**靠近的那個**（就近原則）：

- Either the manager or the employees **are** responsible.（靠近 employees → 複數）
- Either the employees or the manager **is** responsible.（靠近 manager → 單數）
- Neither the director nor his assistants **were** available for comment.（靠近 assistants → 複數）
- Neither the participants nor the lead researcher **was** aware of the confounding variable.（靠近 researcher → 單數）

:::tip
口訣：or / nor → 看右邊（靠近動詞那個）。and → 永遠複數。
:::

### 常見陷阱結構

#### The number of vs A number of

`The number of + 複數名詞` → 動詞**單數**

主詞是 the number（一個數字），本身是單數概念。

- The number of participants **was** limited to fifty.
- The number of studies on this topic **has** grown significantly.
- The number of species in the region **remains** unknown.

`A number of + 複數名詞` → 動詞**複數**

`a number of` 等於 many，後面接複數名詞，動詞跟著複數。

- A number of participants **were** absent from the session.
- A number of factors **contribute** to this phenomenon.
- A number of recent studies **have** challenged this assumption.

#### Each / Every / One of the

`Each / Every + 單數名詞` → 動詞**單數**

- Each country **has** its own policy.
- Every student **is** required to submit a report.
- Each specimen **was** carefully catalogued before analysis.
- Every observation **supports** the central hypothesis.

`One of the + 複數名詞` → 動詞**單數**

主詞是 one，不是後面的複數名詞。

- One of the most important factors **is** temperature.
- One of the researchers **was** awarded the prize.
- One of the experiments **failed** to yield reproducible results.

## 集體名詞（Collective Nouns）

`team / committee / group / government / family / audience / staff / jury` 這類字代表由多人組成的單一群體。

**美式英文（TOEFL 考美式）：集體名詞一律視為單數**，表示群體作為整體採取行動。

- ✓ The committee **has** decided to postpone the vote.
- ✓ The research team **was** conducting experiments in three separate labs.
- ✓ The jury **has** reached a unanimous verdict.
- ✓ The government **is** expected to release a statement tomorrow.
- ✓ The audience **was** captivated by the performance.
- ✗ The committee **have** decided...（英式用法，美式不採用）
- ✗ The team **were** divided in their opinions...（在美式裡應寫 was）

:::note
英式英文有時把集體名詞視為複數（強調成員各自行動），但 TOEFL 採美式標準，集體名詞一律接單數動詞，不必考慮例外。
:::

## 不定代名詞（Indefinite Pronouns）

### 永遠單數 → 動詞單數

`everyone / everybody / someone / somebody / anyone / anybody / no one / nobody / everything / something / anything / nothing / each / either / neither / one`

這些代名詞雖然「感覺像很多人」，但文法上每一個都是單數。

- ✓ Everyone **is** expected to attend the briefing.
- ✓ Neither of the approaches **works** effectively in all contexts.
- ✓ Each of the experiments **was** conducted twice to ensure accuracy.
- ✓ Something **seems** wrong with the data collected in Phase II.
- ✓ No one **has** yet explained the anomaly satisfactorily.
- ✓ Either of the two methods **produces** reliable results.
- ✓ Everybody in the research group **agrees** on the methodology.

### 永遠複數 → 動詞複數

`both / few / many / several / others`

- ✓ Both methods **were** tested under identical conditions.
- ✓ Several of the participants **have** withdrawn from the study.
- ✓ Few **understand** the full implications of these findings.
- ✓ Many **believe** that the current model is insufficient.
- ✓ Others **have** proposed alternative explanations.

### 隨搭配名詞決定（可數複數 or 不可數）

`all / most / some / none / any / half / more / rest`

這組代名詞本身不決定單複數，**動詞跟著 of 後面的名詞走**。

- ✓ Most of the data **is** incomplete.（data 作不可數用）
- ✓ Most of the participants **were** satisfied with the outcome.（participants 複數）
- ✓ All of the evidence **suggests** a strong correlation.（evidence 不可數）
- ✓ All of the samples **were** analyzed within 24 hours.（samples 複數）
- ✓ Some of the information **remains** classified.（information 不可數）
- ✓ Some of the results **were** unexpected.（results 複數）
- ✓ None of the samples **was** usable.（正式書面語，視 none 為單數）
- ✓ None of the samples **were** usable.（口語及一般寫作也接受）
- ✓ Half of the funding **has** been allocated to infrastructure.（funding 不可數）
- ✓ Half of the researchers **were** from overseas institutions.（researchers 複數）
- ✓ The rest of the report **deals** with methodology.（report 單數）
- ✓ The rest of the conclusions **remain** contested.（conclusions 複數）

:::tip
口訣：all/most/some/none/half + of + **名詞**，動詞跟著那個名詞走，不可數→單數，複數名詞→複數。
:::

## There is / There are 結構

`There is/are` 句型中，**動詞看緊接在後的名詞**，而不是後面的修飾語。

- ✓ **There is** a significant difference between the two groups.（difference 單數）
- ✓ **There are** several factors that contribute to this phenomenon.（factors 複數）
- ✓ **There has been** considerable debate about this issue.（debate 不可數）
- ✓ **There were** no significant changes in temperature during the period.（changes 複數）
- ✓ **There remains** one unresolved question in the analysis.（question 單數）
- ✓ **There exist** numerous theories to explain this behavior.（theories 複數，正式文體）

注意 `there is a large number of` 這個常見錯誤：

- ✓ **There is** a large number of studies on this topic.（主詞是 a number → 單數）
- ✓ **There are** a large number of studies on this topic.（口語也可接受）

:::note
學術寫作中，`there exists` 和 `there remains` 是常見的正式替代用法，比 there is/are 更有書面語氣。
:::

## 百分比 / 分數 + of 結構

和不定代名詞的邏輯相同：**動詞跟著 of 後面的名詞走**。

- ✓ Sixty percent of the water **is** contaminated.（water 不可數）
- ✓ Sixty percent of the participants **were** female.（participants 複數）
- ✓ A third of the budget **has** been allocated to research.（budget 不可數）
- ✓ Two-thirds of the experiments **failed** to produce consistent results.（experiments 複數）
- ✓ Approximately 80 percent of the ocean floor **remains** unexplored.（floor 單數）
- ✓ Nearly half of all plant species **are** at risk of extinction.（species 複數）

:::tip
口訣：百分比/分數 + of + 名詞，動詞跟名詞，跟百分比的數字無關。
:::

## 主詞補語不影響動詞

動詞只跟**主詞**一致，be 動詞後面的補語（不管單複數）不影響動詞形式。

- ✓ The main **cause** of the accidents **was** faulty brakes.（主詞是 cause，單數）
- ✓ Her greatest **achievement** **was** the awards she received.（主詞是 achievement，單數）
- ✓ The primary **focus** of the studies **is** long-term behavioral changes.（主詞是 focus）
- ✓ **Photographs** of the excavation site **are** the only evidence we have.（主詞是 photographs，複數）

## 冠詞：a / an / the / 不加

### a / an — 第一次提到、泛指某一個

- I saw **a** bird.（不特定哪隻）
- **A** researcher from Stanford published the study.
- She proposed **a** novel approach to the problem.

`a` vs `an` 看的是**發音**，不是拼字：

- **an** hour（h 不發音，實際開頭是母音 /aʊ/）
- **an** MBA（M 發 /ɛm/，母音開頭）
- **a** university（u 發 /juː/，子音開頭）
- **a** one-time event（o 發 /wʌn/，子音開頭）
- **an** honest mistake（h 不發音）
- **a** European country（E 發 /juː/，子音開頭）

### the — 雙方都知道、世界唯一、前面提過的

- Can you close **the** window?（那扇特定的窗）
- **The** Amazon River is the longest in South America.（世界唯一的那條河）
- I bought a book. **The** book was fascinating.（上一句提過了）
- **The** results of the experiment confirmed the hypothesis.（前文已討論過的那個實驗）

### 不加冠詞 — 泛指抽象概念或複數

說的是「這個東西的整體概念」，而不是某個特定的它：

- **Water** is essential for life.（水這個物質）
- **Researchers** often collaborate across institutions.（研究人員這個群體）
- **Love** is difficult to define.（愛這個抽象概念）
- **Evidence** suggests that early intervention is more effective.（證據這個整體概念）

比較：
- I need **water**.（不可數，泛指） vs. Can you pass me **the** water?（那杯特定的水）
- **Science** has transformed our world.（整個學科） vs. **The** science behind this is complex.（這件事背後的那個科學）

> 冠詞核心邏輯：聽者/讀者知道你指的是哪個嗎？知道 → the；不知道且第一次提 → a/an；說的是整體概念 → 不加。
