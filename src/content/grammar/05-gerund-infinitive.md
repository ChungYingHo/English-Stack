## 基本概念

兩個都可以當「動詞的名詞化」，但有些動詞只能接其中一個。

- **動名詞（Gerund）**：V-ing — She enjoys **reading**.
- **不定詞（Infinitive）**：to + V — She decided **to read**.

---

## 只能接動名詞的動詞

這些動詞後面**只能接 V-ing**，不能接 to V：

- enjoy / finish / avoid / consider / suggest / recommend
- admit / deny / miss / practice / risk / keep / postpone / delay

**avoid** — 避免做某事
- They **avoided using** chemicals that could contaminate the water supply.
- Researchers should **avoid drawing** conclusions without sufficient evidence.
- The team **avoided making** any changes to the original dataset.

**consider** — 考慮做某事
- The team **considered extending** the study by another year.
- Scientists **considered using** a different methodology to control for bias.
- The committee **considered revising** the ethical guidelines.

**finish** — 完成做某事
- The researchers **finished collecting** data from all the sites.
- She finally **finished writing** her dissertation after four years.
- The lab technician **finished calibrating** the instruments before the test began.

**suggest** — 建議做某事
- Experts **suggest incorporating** more renewable energy sources.
- The author **suggests examining** this issue from a different angle.
- The data **suggest considering** alternative explanations.

**recommend** — 推薦做某事
- Physicians **recommend taking** vitamin D during winter months.
- The study **recommends reducing** sugar intake to lower the risk of metabolic disorders.
- Health officials **recommend washing** hands frequently during flu season.

**postpone** — 推遲做某事
- The committee **postponed making** a final decision until more data were available.
- We **postponed publishing** the results until the data were fully verified.
- The conference organizers **postponed holding** the annual meeting due to travel restrictions.

**deny** — 否認做過某事
- The company **denied releasing** toxic waste into the river.
- He **denied having** prior knowledge of the irregularities.
- The officials **denied receiving** any warnings before the incident occurred.

**risk** — 冒著做某事的風險
- Researchers should not **risk contaminating** the samples during handling.
- The team **risked losing** years of data by not backing up their files.
- Without proper precautions, scientists may **risk exposing** themselves to hazardous materials.

**keep** — 持續做某事
- The project **kept running** despite repeated budget cuts.
- Scientists **kept questioning** the original hypothesis even after publication.
- The monitoring system **kept recording** atmospheric data throughout the winter.

**admit** — 承認做過某事
- The researcher **admitted making** an error in the statistical calculation.
- The government **admitted failing** to act early enough on the climate crisis.
- The author **admitted overlooking** several key studies in the original review.

:::tip
記法：這類動詞大多描述「已發生的事」或「習慣性行為」，用 -ing 帶有「正在進行 / 已完成」的感覺。**E-FARMS-DARK** 是常用記憶口訣：Enjoy, Finish, Avoid, Recommend, Mind, Suggest, Deny, Admit, Risk, Keep。
:::

## 只能接不定詞的動詞

這些動詞後面**只能接 to V**：

- want / need / decide / plan / hope / expect / agree / refuse / manage
- attempt / tend / fail / promise / afford / claim / pretend

**decide** — 決定做某事
- The committee **decided to fund** the project for another three years.
- Scientists **decided to replicate** the experiment under different environmental conditions.
- The editor **decided to reject** the manuscript due to insufficient evidence.

**agree** — 同意做某事
- All participants **agreed to sign** the consent form before the study began.
- The two countries **agreed to reduce** carbon emissions by 30% within a decade.
- The researchers **agreed to share** their raw data with the international team.

**refuse** — 拒絕做某事
- The company **refused to release** the internal safety data to regulators.
- Several participants **refused to continue** after learning the full risks involved.
- The journal **refused to publish** the findings without independent replication.

**manage** — 設法做到某事
- Despite the obstacles, the team **managed to complete** the study on time.
- Researchers **managed to identify** the source of the contamination after months of testing.
- The engineers **managed to reduce** energy consumption by 40% through design improvements.

**tend** — 傾向於做某事
- Larger animals **tend to have** longer lifespans than smaller ones.
- Peer-reviewed studies **tend to receive** more attention and citation.
- Invasive species **tend to outcompete** native species in nutrient-rich environments.

**fail** — 未能做到某事
- The experiment **failed to produce** consistent results across all three trials.
- Researchers **failed to account** for seasonal variation in their initial model.
- The new drug **failed to demonstrate** a statistically significant improvement over the placebo.

**claim** — 聲稱做到某事
- The company **claimed to have** developed a breakthrough treatment for Alzheimer's.
- He **claimed to know** nothing about the falsified data.
- The researchers **claimed to have** observed the phenomenon under controlled conditions.

**promise** — 承諾做某事
- The government **promised to reduce** emissions by 40% before 2030.
- The researcher **promised to submit** the revised manuscript by the end of the month.
- The institution **promised to provide** additional funding if the pilot study succeeded.

**afford** — 負擔得起做某事
- Without additional funding, they could not **afford to continue** the project.
- Few universities can **afford to maintain** such large research facilities independently.
- The team could not **afford to ignore** the outliers in the dataset.

:::tip
記法：這類動詞大多描述「未來的意圖、計畫或結果」，`to V` 帶有「指向未來」的方向感。
:::

## 兩者都可以，但意思不同

這是最難的部分，同一個動詞接 -ing 和 to V 意思完全不一樣。

### remember

- **remember to V** — 記得去做（還沒做，提醒未來）
  - Please **remember to submit** the form by Friday.
  - Researchers should **remember to document** every step of the process.

- **remember V-ing** — 記得曾經做過（已做過，回憶過去）
  - I **remember reading** this paper before, but I cannot recall the conclusion.
  - She **remembered seeing** the anomaly in the data, but said nothing at the time.

### forget

- **forget to V** — 忘記要去做（沒做，失誤）
  - She **forgot to bring** the samples to the lab.
  - The assistant **forgot to turn off** the centrifuge after the experiment.

- **forget V-ing** — 忘記曾經做過（已做過，卻不記得了）
  - He **forgot meeting** her at the conference last year.
  - I'll never **forget seeing** the northern lights for the first time.

### stop

- **stop to V** — 停下來，去做另一件事（目的）
  - The researcher **stopped to check** the equipment midway through the trial.（停下手邊的事去檢查）
  - She **stopped to read** the notice posted on the lab door.

- **stop V-ing** — 停止做某件事（終止）
  - The researcher **stopped checking** the data after the third verification.（停止做檢查這件事）
  - The factory **stopped releasing** pollutants after the new regulations took effect.

### try

- **try to V** — 試圖做（有難度，努力嘗試）
  - Scientists **tried to replicate** the results but were unable to do so.（努力嘗試但失敗）
  - The team **tried to control** for all confounding variables.

- **try V-ing** — 試試看（嘗試一種方法，看看效果）
  - **Try adjusting** the temperature and see if it changes the reaction rate.（試試這個方法）
  - If the standard protocol fails, **try reducing** the concentration.

## 介系詞後面一律接動名詞

介系詞（in / by / for / about / without / after / before / of...）後面**只能接 V-ing**，不能接 to V。

- She succeeded **in solving** the problem through a novel approach.
- **Without knowing** the cause, we cannot find a cure.
- The results were obtained **by analyzing** satellite data over a ten-year period.
- **After conducting** three experiments, the team reached a consistent conclusion.
- The researchers are interested **in exploring** the long-term effects of the treatment.
- The new method is capable **of detecting** concentrations as low as one part per billion.

常見錯誤：

- ✗ She succeeded **in to solve** the problem.
- ✓ She succeeded **in solving** the problem.

- ✗ The results were obtained **by to analyze** the data.
- ✓ The results were obtained **by analyzing** the data.

## 主詞位置

V-ing 和 to V 都可以當主詞，但風格不同：

- **Studying** abroad broadens your perspective.（動名詞當主詞，較口語、直接）
- **To study** abroad is a valuable experience.（不定詞當主詞，較正式）

TOEFL 寫作中用 `It is + adj + to V` 更常見：

- **It is** essential **to** understand the context before drawing conclusions.
- **It is** difficult **to** predict long-term outcomes in complex ecosystems.
- **It is** important **to** distinguish between correlation and causation.

## 使役動詞 + 受詞 + V（原形，不加 to）

`make / let / have / help` 後面接「受詞 + 動詞原形」，不加 to：

### make — 強迫某人做

- The new regulations **made** researchers **reconsider** their methodology entirely.
- The discovery **made** scientists **question** the validity of the existing theory.
- The pressure from funding agencies **made** the team **rush** their publication.

### let — 允許某人做

- The university **let** the team **continue** the research despite the budget cuts.
- The editor **let** the authors **revise** the paper before final submission.
- The institution **let** junior researchers **lead** certain aspects of the study.

### have — 安排或指示某人做

- The professor **had** the students **analyze** three case studies in detail.
- The lab director **had** the technicians **recalibrate** the instruments before the trial.
- The senior researcher **had** her assistant **compile** all the relevant literature.

### help — 幫助某人做（to 可加可不加）

- The new software **helped** researchers **process** data more efficiently.
- Collaboration **helped** the team **achieve** results faster than expected.
- Clear methodology **helped** reviewers **evaluate** the study's validity.

:::tip
注意：`get someone to do` 中的 `to` 要保留：
- The department **got** the committee **to approve** the revised budget.
- The lead researcher **got** her team **to adopt** a new data format.
:::

## 感官動詞 + 受詞 + V / V-ing

`see / hear / feel / watch / notice / observe` + 受詞後，可以接原形動詞或 V-ing，意思不同：

- 接 **V（原形）** → 看到/聽到完整的動作（從頭到尾，完整發生）
- 接 **V-ing** → 看到/聽到動作正在進行中（當下片段）

- She **saw** the glacier **collapse** into the ocean.（看到整個崩塌過程，完整）
- She **saw** the glacier **collapsing** slowly.（看到它正在崩塌中，進行中）

- Scientists **observed** the cells **divide** under the microscope.（觀察到完整的細胞分裂過程）
- Scientists **observed** the cells **dividing** rapidly.（觀察到細胞正在快速分裂）

- The researcher **heard** the machine **stop** suddenly.（聽到機器停下來的那一刻）
- The researcher **heard** the machine **making** a strange noise.（聽到機器持續發出聲音）
