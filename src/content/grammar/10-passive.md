## 為什麼用被動語態

學術文章大量使用被動語態，原因是：
1. **執行者不重要或不知道**：重點是「做了什麼」不是「誰做的」
2. **讓文章更客觀**：避免用第一人稱（we/I）
3. **強調受詞/結果**：把重點移到動作承受者

- The samples **were collected** from three different sites.（不強調誰收集的）
- The theory **was proposed** in 1905.（重點是理論本身，不是誰提出的）
- Further research **is needed**.（客觀陳述）

---

## 各時態的被動形式

公式：**be + p.p.**，be 動詞隨時態變化

| 時態 | 主動 | 被動 |
|------|------|------|
| 現在式 | collects | is/are collected |
| 過去式 | collected | was/were collected |
| 未來式 | will collect | will be collected |
| 現在完成 | has/have collected | has/have been collected |
| 過去完成 | had collected | had been collected |
| 現在進行 | is/are collecting | is/are being collected |
| 過去進行 | was/were collecting | was/were being collected |
| 情態動詞 | must/should/can collect | must/should/can be collected |

例句涵蓋每個時態：
- The data **are collected** every six months.（現在式）
- The samples **were stored** at −20°C.（過去式）
- The results **will be published** next year.（未來式）
- The hypothesis **has been tested** in three separate studies.（現在完成）
- By the time the team arrived, the specimens **had been contaminated**.（過去完成）
- The experiment **is currently being conducted** at two research centers.（現在進行）
- Participants **were being observed** throughout the entire process.（過去進行）
- The protocol **must be** followed exactly.（情態動詞）
- Further testing **should be** done before drawing conclusions.（情態動詞）

---

## by + 執行者

執行者重要時，用 **by** 引出：
- The theory of evolution **was proposed by** Charles Darwin **in 1859**.
- The samples **were analyzed by** three independent labs.
- The report **was reviewed by** the editorial board before publication.

執行者不重要時，省略 by 片語（這是被動的主要用途）：
- The city **was founded in** 1632.（沒必要說是誰建立的）
- The data **were collected** over a ten-year period.

---

## 含有雙受詞的被動（teach / give / show / tell / send / offer）

主動句有兩個受詞（直接受詞 + 間接受詞）時，兩種都可以當被動句主詞：

- The team **gave** the committee a detailed report.
  → The committee **was given** a detailed report.（間接受詞當主詞）
  → A detailed report **was given** to the committee.（直接受詞當主詞）
- Scientists **showed** the participants a series of images.
  → The participants **were shown** a series of images.

---

## 分詞的被動用法（Participial Passive）

用於簡化句子，常見於 TOEFL 閱讀。

**過去分詞放句首**（等同 having been + p.p.，常省略 having been）：
- **Conducted over three years**, the study provides reliable data.（= The study, which was conducted over three years, ...）
- **Founded in 1636**, Harvard is the oldest university in the United States.
- **Written in 1859**, The Origin of Species changed scientific thinking.

**Being + p.p.**（強調「正在被做」）：
- The specimen, **being examined** under the microscope, showed unusual properties.
- The data, **being collected** from remote regions, required special equipment.

**Having been + p.p.**（強調「已經被做完了才…」）：
- **Having been tested** multiple times, the method was considered reliable.
- **Having been approved** by the ethics board, the study could begin.

---

## Get 被動（非正式，但 TOEFL 閱讀偶爾出現）

**get + p.p.** — 強調「意外發生的事」或「過程的完成」：
- The samples **got contaminated** during transit.（意外被污染）
- The data **got lost** in the system crash.（意外遺失）
- The paper finally **got published** after two years of revisions.（終於被出版）

:::tip
學術文章用 be + p.p.（正式），口語和一般文章用 get + p.p.（非正式）。TOEFL 寫作裡用 be + p.p.。
:::
