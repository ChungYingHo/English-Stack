## 可數 vs 不可數名詞

不可數名詞**不加 s，不用 a/an，動詞單數**。

常見不可數名詞：information / evidence / research / data / equipment / advice / knowledge / progress / work / news / feedback

- ✓ The **research** shows that...（不加 s）
- ✗ The researches show that...

- ✓ **Much** evidence supports this. / **Little** information is available.
- ✗ Many evidences / Few informations

量化不可數名詞用 `a piece of` / `an amount of` / `a lack of`：

- a piece of **advice**
- a lack of **evidence**

### data 的特殊性

學術英文中 data 通常視為**複數**（datum 的複數）：

- ✓ The data **were** collected over three years.

### 同一個字，可數 / 不可數意思不同

某些名詞在不同語境下，可數和不可數都有，但意義不同：

- **work**（不可數：工作/作品的概念）vs. **works**（可數：作品，如 works of art）
- **experience**（不可數：經驗）vs. **an experience**（可數：一段經歷）
- **paper**（不可數：紙）vs. **a paper**（可數：一篇論文）
- **room**（不可數：空間）vs. **a room**（可數：一個房間）
- **glass**（不可數：玻璃）vs. **a glass**（可數：一個玻璃杯）

### 不可數名詞的常見量化方式

- a piece of advice / information / evidence / news / furniture
- a body of evidence / research / knowledge
- a set of data / guidelines / rules
- an amount of water / money / time
- a lack of evidence / resources / interest

## 比較級 & 最高級

### 形式

- 短字（1-2 音節）→ `-er / -est`：faster, the fastest
- 長字（3 音節以上）→ `more / most`：more significant, the most significant

- ✓ This method is **more effective** than the previous one.
- ✗ This method is **more effectiver** than...

### 常見搭配

- `比較級 + than`：taller **than**
- `as + 原級 + as`：as tall **as**
- `the + 最高級 + of/in`：the tallest **in** the class

### 修飾比較級

- **much / far / significantly** + 比較級（強調差距大）
- The new model is **significantly faster** than the old one.

### 雙重比較級（The + 比較級, the + 比較級）

表示「越…，越…」：

- **The more** data researchers collect, **the more** accurate their conclusions become.
- **The longer** the study continues, **the more** reliable the results are.
- **The higher** the temperature, **the faster** the reaction occurs.
- **The fewer** variables involved, **the clearer** the analysis becomes.

### 倍數比較

- This method is **twice as fast as** the previous one.
- The new sample size is **three times larger than** the original.
- CO₂ levels are now **more than 40% higher than** pre-industrial levels.

### 否定 as...as

- The results are **not as conclusive as** researchers had hoped.
- The new treatment is **not as effective as** the original drug.
- ✓ not as + adj. + as（否定同等比較）

### 比較的對象要對稱（Illogical Comparison）

- ✗ The temperature in summer is higher than **winter**.（溫度 vs 冬天，不對稱）
- ✓ The temperature in summer is higher than **in winter** / **that in winter**.
- ✗ The results of this study are better than **the previous study**.（結果 vs 研究，不對稱）
- ✓ The results of this study are better than **those of the previous study**.

### 形容詞 vs 副詞（常見混用）

- ✗ The experiment went **smooth**. → ✓ The experiment went **smoothly**.（副詞修飾動詞）
- ✗ She works **real** hard. → ✓ She works **really** hard.

連綴動詞（feel / seem / look / sound / taste / appear / become）後接**形容詞**，不接副詞：

- The data **seem** reliable.（不是 reliably）
- The situation **became** complicated.（不是 complicatedly）
- The new method **looks** promising.（不是 promisingly）

## 條件句

### 真實條件（可能發生）

`If + 現在式, will + V`

- If temperatures **rise**, sea levels **will increase**.

### 假設條件（不太可能 / 與現在相反）

`If + 過去式, would + V`

為什麼用「過去式」表達假設？因為英文用時態的「距離」來表達「跟現實的距離」。過去式代表「退一步、不是真的」，不是真的在說過去的事。

- If humans **stopped** burning fossil fuels, the climate **would stabilize**.
  （實際上人類並沒有停止，這是假設）
- If I **were** you, I **would** reconsider.（were 不是過去式，是假設語氣）

### 與過去事實相反

`If + had + p.p., would have + p.p.`

- If they **had collected** more data, the results **would have been** more reliable.

### 混合條件句（過去條件 + 現在結果）

- If the industrial revolution **had occurred** a century later, modern pollution levels **would** still be rising.
- If scientists **had discovered** antibiotics earlier, millions **would** be alive today.

### Wish 假設句

- **I wish** the sample size **were** larger.（wish + 過去式 = 希望現在不同）
- **I wish** they **had published** the results earlier.（wish + 過去完成 = 希望過去不同）
- Scientists **wish** there **were** more funding for basic research.

> 看到 would/could/might，大概率是假設語氣，動詞形式跟著改。

## 被動語態（精簡版）

`be + p.p.`，強調動作結果，或不知道/不重要是誰做的。

TOEFL 學術文章大量使用被動，因為重點是「研究發現」不是「誰發現」：

- The samples **are stored** at −20°C.（現在習慣）
- The data **were analyzed** using statistical software.（過去）
- The hypothesis **has been** supported by multiple studies.（現在完成）
- The results **will be** published in a peer-reviewed journal.（未來）
- The experiment **is being** conducted under controlled conditions.（現在進行中）
- The specimens **were being** examined when the power went out.（過去進行）

### 情態動詞 + 被動

- The protocol **must be** followed exactly.
- Further research **should be** conducted.
- Additional data **may be** required.
- The findings **could be** replicated in other settings.

### by + 執行者

- The theory **was proposed by** Einstein.（執行者重要時才加）
- The samples **were collected by** three independent research teams.
- The report **was published by** the World Health Organization.

> 被動語態的完整整理（各時態表格、分詞被動、get 被動）請參見 Grammar 10。

## 常混淆的字

### affect vs effect

- **affect** — 動詞：Climate change **affects** wildlife.
- **effect** — 名詞：The **effects** of climate change are severe.

記法：**A**ffect = **A**ction（動詞），**E**ffect = **E**nd result（名詞）

### rise vs raise

- **rise**（rose / risen）— 不及物，自己升高，後面不加受詞
  - Temperatures **rise** in summer.
- **raise**（raised / raised）— 及物，把某物升高，後面要加受詞
  - The government **raised** the minimum wage.

### lie vs lay

- **lie**（lay / lain）— 不及物，躺；也有「說謊」的意思（lied / lied）
  - The patient **lay** on the bed.（過去式）
- **lay**（laid / laid）— 及物，放置某物
  - She **laid** the documents on the table.

### increase / decrease 的用法

- ✓ The temperature **increased** by 2°C.（動詞，不加 to）
- ✓ There was an **increase** of 2°C.（名詞）
- ✓ The population **rose to** 10 million.（rise to = 升至某數字）

### because vs because of vs due to vs owing to vs since vs as

- **because** + 子句：**Because** the sample was small, results were inconclusive.
- **because of** + 名詞：The study was delayed **because of** equipment failure.
- **due to** + 名詞（較正式）：The project was cancelled **due to** lack of funding.
- **owing to** + 名詞（最正式）：**Owing to** budget constraints, the team was reduced.
- **since / as** + 子句（解釋原因，語氣較弱）：**Since** resources were limited, the team prioritized key variables.
- ✗ Due to **the equipment failed**...（due to 不接子句）

### despite vs although vs in spite of

- **despite** + 名詞/動名詞：**Despite** the challenges, the study was completed.
- **in spite of** + 名詞/動名詞：**In spite of** limited funding, the project succeeded.
- **although** + 子句：**Although** funding was limited, the project succeeded.
- ✗ Despite **the funding was limited**...（despite 不接子句）
- ✓ Despite **the limited funding**...（接名詞）

### number vs amount vs quantity

- **number** — 可數名詞的數量：the number of participants
- **amount** — 不可數名詞的量：the amount of data
- **quantity** — 可數或不可數皆可（較正式/技術性）：a large quantity of carbon dioxide

### that vs which（限定 vs 非限定子句）

- **that** — 限定子句，不加逗號，不能修飾整個句子
- **which** — 可限定（不加逗號）或非限定（加逗號）；也可以指前面整個子句
  - ✓ The experiment succeeded, **which** surprised everyone.（which 指前面整件事）
  - ✗ The experiment succeeded, **that** surprised everyone.（that 不能這樣用）

### few vs a few vs little vs a little

- **few** + 可數（負面）= 幾乎沒有
  - **Few** studies have examined this question.（幾乎沒有研究）
- **a few** + 可數（正面）= 有一些，幾個
  - **A few** studies have shown promising results.（有幾個研究）
- **little** + 不可數（負面）= 幾乎沒有
  - There is **little** evidence to support this claim.
- **a little** + 不可數（正面）= 有一點點
  - There is **a little** evidence suggesting a correlation.

## 數字與量詞快速整理

- **fewer** + 可數複數：fewer students
- **less** + 不可數：less time, less water
- **many** + 可數複數：many studies
- **much** + 不可數：much research
- **several** — 幾個（比 a few 多，比 many 少），只接可數複數
  - **Several** factors contribute to this phenomenon.
- **numerous** — 許多（正式，比 many 更學術）
  - **Numerous** studies have confirmed this finding.
- **a great deal of / a large amount of** — 大量（接不可數）
  - **A great deal of** research has been devoted to this question.
- **a large number of / a great number of** — 大量（接可數複數）
  - **A large number of** experiments were conducted.
- **the majority of** — 多數（接複數名詞，動詞通常複數）
  - **The majority of** participants **were** satisfied with the results.
- **a variety of / a range of** — 各種各樣（接複數名詞）
  - **A variety of** methods were employed.
- **a series of** — 一系列（series 本身單數，動詞單數）
  - **A series of** experiments was conducted.

## 快速檢查清單

托福寫作或閱讀碰到文法困惑時，依序問自己：

1. **主詞是誰？** 是單數還是複數？
2. **時間點？** 過去特定時間還是持續到現在？
3. **是誰做的？** 還是被做的？（主動/被動）
4. **連接詞對了嗎？** 兩邊結構是否平行？
5. **可數還是不可數？** 量詞選對了嗎？
