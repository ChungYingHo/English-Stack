## 情態動詞的共同規則

情態動詞（Modal Verbs）：can / could / may / might / will / would / shall / should / must / ought to

共同規則：
- 後面接**動詞原形**（不加 to，不加 s）
- 本身**不隨主詞變化**（沒有 he mights / she cans）
- 否定：modal + **not** + V

---

## 必要性與義務

### must — 強烈義務（說話者認為絕對必要）

- All participants **must** sign the consent form before the study begins.
- You **must** not tamper with the samples.（mustn't = 絕對禁止）
- Data **must** be stored securely and backed up daily.

### have to — 外部規定、客觀義務

- Researchers **have to** follow the ethical guidelines set by the institution.（規定如此）
- She **doesn't have to** attend the optional seminar.（不需要，沒有義務）
- All submissions **have to** include a conflict-of-interest declaration.

`mustn't` = 禁止做；`don't have to` = 不需要做（但可以做）

:::tip
陷阱：`must not` 是「絕對禁止」，`don't have to` 是「不必要」，兩者意思完全不同。
- You **must not** falsify data.（禁止）
- You **don't have to** submit a draft in advance.（不必，但可以）
:::

### should / ought to — 建議、應該（但不強制）

- Scientists **should** consider potential biases when designing experiments.
- You **ought to** review the methodology before proceeding to data collection.
- Researchers **should** disclose any funding sources that may create a conflict of interest.
- The team **ought to** replicate the study before publishing the findings.

---

## 可能性與推測

這是 TOEFL 閱讀最常出現的情態動詞用法，表達「對現在或過去的推測」。

### must — 幾乎確定（邏輯推斷）

- The temperature is dropping rapidly. There **must** be a system failure.（幾乎確定）
- The isotope ratio **must** indicate a different geological period.
- Given the evidence, the population **must** have declined rapidly.

### may / might — 可能（不確定）

- The new treatment **may** reduce side effects in elderly patients.（可能，較正式）
- Further testing **might** reveal additional factors that were not initially apparent.（可能，較謹慎）
- The anomaly **may** be explained by variations in temperature during the sampling period.

兩者意思相近，`might` 比 `may` 的確定程度稍低一點。

### could — 理論上的可能性

- This finding **could** have significant implications for climate policy.
- The anomaly **could** be explained by several independent factors.
- Changes in ocean salinity **could** disrupt established migration patterns.

> 托福閱讀常用 `may / might / could` 來表達學術上的謹慎態度，不輕易斷言。

### can't / couldn't — 幾乎不可能

- That **can't** be the right explanation — the data directly contradict it.
- The sample size **couldn't** account for all the observed variation.
- A single gene **can't** be responsible for such a complex behavioral trait.

### 推測的強度排列

確定 → 不確定：**must** > **should** > **may / might** > **could**

## 過去的推測

情態動詞 + **have + p.p.** = 對過去的推測：

- The experiment **must have been** conducted under controlled conditions.（幾乎確定）
- The anomaly **must have been** caused by a calibration error that went undetected.（幾乎確定）
- The data **may have been** affected by external variables during the sampling period.（可能）
- The temperature **may have fluctuated** significantly during the night.（可能）
- The researcher **might have overlooked** this critical variable in the initial analysis.（也許）
- The team **could have prevented** the contamination with proper protocols in place.（本可以，但沒有）
- They **should have tested** the hypothesis under different environmental conditions.（本應該，但沒做）
- The experiment **ought to have been** completed months ago, but delays in funding held it back.（本應完成，但沒完成）
- She **couldn't have known** about the error at that point in the research process.（不可能知道）
- The results **would have been** more accurate with a larger and more diverse sample.（假設語氣）

## will vs would

### will — 預測、承諾、事實

- The results **will** be published in the next issue of the journal.
- If we increase the temperature by 10°C, the reaction **will** accelerate noticeably.
- Further analysis **will** determine whether the findings can be generalized.

### would — 假設、委婉、過去的習慣

- If I **were** a researcher, I **would** focus on the genetics of aging.（假設）
- **Would** you mind explaining the methodology in more detail?（委婉請求）
- As a child, she **would** spend hours in the library reading science magazines.（過去的習慣）
- A larger sample size **would** have improved the reliability of the results.（假設）

## had better / would rather / be supposed to

### had better — 強烈建議（帶有警告意味）

`had better` 用來給出強烈建議，暗示如果不做可能會有不好的後果，語氣比 `should` 更強：

- Researchers **had better** verify the data before publication, or risk retractions.
- You **had better not** ignore these warning signs in the experimental results.
- The team **had better** back up all data before the server maintenance begins.

:::tip
注意：`had better` 後面直接接動詞原形，不加 `to`。
- ✓ You **had better** review the protocol.
- ✗ You **had better to** review the protocol.
否定形式：`had better not + V`（中間不加 do）
:::

### would rather — 傾向選擇（相較之下更想要）

`would rather` 表示在兩個選項中傾向選擇某一個：

- Scientists **would rather** conduct longer studies than rely on short-term data.
- The committee **would rather** delay the decision than make an error at this stage.
- I **would rather** not comment on the preliminary findings before peer review.
- Researchers **would rather** publish in open-access journals **than** restrict access to their work.

**would rather...than** 結構（兩者對比）：
- Scientists **would rather** gather more evidence **than** speculate without sufficient data.
- The team **would rather** start over **than** submit flawed results.

否定形式：`would rather not + V`：
- The lead author **would rather not** respond to the allegations publicly.

### be supposed to — 按照計畫、規定或預期應該

`be supposed to` 表示根據計畫、規定、預期或慣例「應該」如何，常帶有「但實際上不一定這樣」的暗示：

- The results **are supposed to be** published next month, pending final review.（有這個計畫）
- Participants **were supposed to** complete all three sessions, but attrition was high.（有此要求，但未必做到）
- The experiment **was supposed to** last six months, but it was cut short due to funding issues.（原本計畫如此，但實際上沒有）
- Researchers **are supposed to** declare all conflicts of interest upon submission.（規定如此）

:::tip
`be supposed to` vs `should`：
- `should` = 我建議你應該做（主觀建議）
- `be supposed to` = 按照規定/計畫/別人的期待你應該做（外部規範）
:::

## 學術文章中的情態動詞

TOEFL 學術文章中，情態動詞常用來表達「謹慎態度」，避免過度絕對的陳述：

- ✗ This proves that... → ✓ This **may** suggest that...
- ✗ The results show... → ✓ The results **could** indicate...
- ✓ Future research **should** examine this relationship in more diverse populations.
- ✓ This **might** have implications for how we approach conservation policy.
- ✓ The findings **may** support the hypothesis, but further replication is needed.

看到這類表達，要理解作者刻意在保留不確定性，不是在說「一定」。這是學術寫作「hedge（謹慎表達）」的核心技巧。
