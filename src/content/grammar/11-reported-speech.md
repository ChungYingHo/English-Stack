## 直接引語 vs 間接引語

**直接引語（Direct Speech）**：直接引用說話者的原話，用引號：
- Darwin **said**, "Natural selection is the mechanism of evolution."
- The researcher **stated**, "The data support the hypothesis."

**間接引語（Reported/Indirect Speech）**：轉述別人說的話，不用引號，時態通常後移：
- Darwin **said that** natural selection **was** the mechanism of evolution.
- The researcher **stated that** the data **supported** the hypothesis.

---

## 時態後移規則（Backshift）

間接引語通常把時態「往後推一格」：

| 直接引語 | 間接引語 |
|---------|---------|
| 現在式 → | 過去式 |
| 現在完成式 → | 過去完成式 |
| 過去式 → | 過去完成式 |
| will → | would |
| can → | could |
| may → | might |
| must → | had to |
| am/is/are + V-ing → | was/were + V-ing |

例句：
- "The experiment **is** successful." → She said the experiment **was** successful.
- "We **have found** a new species." → They announced they **had found** a new species.
- "The results **were** unexpected." → The paper reported that the results **had been** unexpected.
- "We **will** publish the findings." → The team said they **would** publish the findings.
- "Researchers **can** replicate the method." → They noted that researchers **could** replicate the method.
- "The data **may** be affected." → The author warned that the data **might** be affected.

**不後移的情況（永遠成立的事實或定律）**：
- "Water **boils** at 100°C." → The textbook states that water **boils** at 100°C.（科學事實，保持現在式）
- "The Earth **revolves** around the Sun." → Scientists proved that the Earth **revolves** around the Sun.

## say vs tell vs ask

- **say (to)** — 說，不需要直接受詞（說話對象）
  - She **said** the results were promising.
  - She **said to** the team that the results were promising.
  - ✗ She **said** them the results were promising.

- **tell** — 告訴某人，必須有直接受詞
  - She **told** the team that the results were promising.
  - ✗ She **told** that the results were promising.（缺受詞）

- **ask** — 問；請求
  - He **asked** whether the method was reliable.（疑問）
  - She **asked** the team **to review** the data.（請求 = ask someone to do）

---

## 疑問句的間接引語

原本的疑問句語序，在間接引語中改為**陳述句語序**（主詞 + 動詞）：

**Yes/No 問句 → whether / if 子句**：
- "Is the hypothesis valid?" → They asked **whether the hypothesis was** valid.
- "Did the experiment succeed?" → She asked **if the experiment had succeeded**.
- ✗ She asked **whether was the hypothesis** valid.（不能保留問句語序）

**Wh- 問句 → wh- 子句（陳述語序）**：
- "What **does** the data **show**?" → Researchers asked what the data **showed**.
- "Why **did** the experiment **fail**?" → They wanted to know why the experiment **had failed**.
- "How **long** does the process **take**?" → She asked how long the process **took**.
- ✗ She asked how long **does** the process **take**.（不能保留問句語序）

## 常用報導動詞（Reporting Verbs）

學術文章中，比 say/tell 更精確的報導動詞：

**中立報導**：state / report / indicate / note / observe / mention / describe
- The study **reports** that ocean temperatures have risen by 0.5°C.
- The author **notes** that several limitations exist.

**表達論點/主張**：argue / claim / contend / maintain / assert / insist
- The researcher **argues** that current models are insufficient.
- The paper **contends** that earlier estimates were too conservative.

**表達發現/結論**：find / discover / reveal / demonstrate / show / conclude / determine
- The study **found** that exposure to green spaces reduces stress.
- Researchers **demonstrated** that the treatment was effective.
- The data **reveal** a clear correlation between income and health outcomes.

**表達建議/要求**：suggest / recommend / propose / urge / emphasize
- The author **suggests** that further research is needed.
- Experts **recommend** that governments take immediate action.
- The committee **proposed** that the guidelines be revised.

**表達質疑/否定**：question / challenge / dispute / deny / contradict / refute
- Several scientists **questioned** whether the sample was representative.
- The new evidence **challenges** the traditional view.
- The study **refutes** the claim that caffeine impairs memory.

**表達同意**：agree / acknowledge / confirm / support / accept
- Most researchers **agree** that climate change is human-driven.
- The findings **support** the hypothesis that diet affects cognition.

:::tip
TOEFL 寫作中使用多樣的報導動詞，比一直用 say 更能展示詞彙能力。
:::
