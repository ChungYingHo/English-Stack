## 什麼是子句？

子句（clause）= **有主詞 + 有動詞**的結構。跟片語（phrase）的差別是：片語沒有主詞或動詞。

- 片語：*the scientist with red hair*（沒有動詞）
- 子句：*the scientist **who has** red hair*（有主詞 who + 動詞 has）

一個句子可以有多個子句，用連接詞或關係代名詞串起來。

---

## 關係子句（Relative Clauses）

用來修飾名詞，說明「哪一個」或「補充說明」。

### who / that — 修飾人

- Scientists **who study** marine life often dive deep.
- The researcher **that conducted** the study is from MIT.
- Children **who grow up** in bilingual households often develop stronger cognitive flexibility.

### which / that — 修飾事物

- The theory, **which was proposed in 1905**, changed physics.
- The data **that we collected** support this hypothesis.
- The glacier **that has retreated the most** is located in the Alps.
- A protein **which regulates** cell division was identified by the research team.

### whose — 表所屬

- Scientists **whose research** focuses on genetics are in high demand.
- A species **whose habitat** has been destroyed faces a higher risk of extinction.
- The laboratory **whose equipment** was damaged had to suspend operations for three months.

### where — 修飾地點名詞

用 `where` 代替 `in which / at which`，修飾表示地點的名詞：

- The lab **where** the experiments were conducted has since been closed.
- A region **where** biodiversity is especially high requires stronger conservation efforts.
- The ocean floor **where** hydrothermal vents exist supports unique ecosystems.

### when — 修飾時間名詞

用 `when` 代替 `at which / during which`，修飾表示時間的名詞：

- The 1960s, **when** space exploration began, was a transformative era.
- There are moments **when** even the best equipment fails.
- The period **when** dinosaurs dominated the Earth lasted over 160 million years.

### why — 修飾 reason

- The reason **why** the experiment failed is still unclear.
- ✓ The reason **that** it failed remains under investigation.（that 也可以）
- ✓ The reason it failed remains under investigation.（省略也正確，最常用）

### whom — 修飾人（受格，正式書面語）

`whom` 是 `who` 的受格形式，在正式文章中用於受詞位置：

- The researcher **whom** we interviewed last week has published her findings.
- The scientists **with whom** the team collaborated are from Germany.
- The professor **to whom** the award was given has spent thirty years studying climate change.

:::tip
口訣：用 `whom` 的地方，可以代換成 `him/her`（受格）；能代換成 `he/she`（主格）則用 `who`。
- We interviewed **her** → The researcher **whom** we interviewed ✓
:::

### 限定 vs 非限定

`which` 加逗號 → **補充說明**（拿掉也沒關係）

`that` 沒逗號 → **限定說明**（說明是哪一個，不能拿掉）

- The experiment **that failed** → 特指那個失敗的實驗（限定）
- The experiment, **which failed**, ... → 補充提到它失敗了（非限定）
- Students **who fail to submit on time** will receive a penalty.（限定：特指那些未交作業的學生）
- The professor, **who has published over 200 papers**, will retire next year.（非限定：補充說明教授的背景）
- The glacier **that has retreated the most** is located in the Alps.（限定：特指那一條）
- The Rhône Glacier, **which has retreated significantly**, is located in Switzerland.（非限定：額外說明）

:::tip
非限定關係子句（帶逗號的 which）可以整段拿掉，主句仍然完整且意思清楚。這正是 TOEFL 閱讀中常見的「插入語」技巧。
:::

## 簡化關係子句（Reduced Relative Clauses）

這是 TOEFL 閱讀最常見的結構之一，把關係子句的「關係代名詞 + be 動詞」省略，留下分詞修飾語。

### 主動 — 保留 V-ing

原來的關係子句是主動語態，去掉「關係代名詞 + be 動詞」後留 V-ing：

- The scientist **who is leading** the project → The scientist **leading** the project
- Researchers **who are studying** ocean temperatures → Researchers **studying** ocean temperatures
- Factors **that contribute** to global warming → Factors **contributing** to global warming
- Animals **that live** in extreme environments → Animals **living** in extreme environments

### 被動 — 保留 p.p.

原來的關係子句是被動語態，去掉「關係代名詞 + be 動詞」後留過去分詞：

- The data **that were collected** at three sites → The data **collected** at three sites
- A theory **that was proposed** in 1905 → A theory **proposed** in 1905
- Species **that are found** only in the tropics → Species **found** only in the tropics
- The method **that was adopted** by most researchers → The method **adopted** by most researchers

### 判斷主動或被動

- 原本是主動（名詞「做」這個動作）→ 用 V-ing
- 原本是被動（名詞「被」這個動作施加）→ 用 p.p.

辨認練習：

- *The method **adopted** by most researchers* = which was adopted（被動）
- *Water **flowing** through the canyon* = which flows（主動）
- *The paper **published** last year* = which was published（被動）
- *Scientists **working** in the field* = who are working（主動）

:::tip
簡化關係子句是 TOEFL 長難句的核心。遇到「名詞 + V-ing/p.p.」的結構，先判斷它是不是簡化的關係子句，再還原成完整子句幫助理解。
:::

## 同位語片語（Appositive Phrases）

同位語是放在名詞後面、用逗號隔開的名詞片語，用來補充說明或定義前面的名詞，功能相當於非限定關係子句的精簡版。

- Charles Darwin, **the naturalist who proposed the theory of evolution**, spent five years studying wildlife.
- The Amazon River, **the world's largest river by discharge**, runs through Brazil.
- Silicon dioxide, **the main component of sand and glass**, has a melting point of 1,710°C.
- The team used CRISPR, **a gene-editing technology developed in 2012**, to modify the cells.
- Mount Everest, **the highest peak on Earth**, attracts thousands of climbers each year.

### 同位語的特徵

- 放在被解釋名詞之後，用逗號隔開
- 可以直接拿掉，主句仍然完整
- 通常是「名詞片語」（不含主動詞）
- TOEFL 閱讀中大量出現，要能快速跳過同位語找到主詞

:::tip
TOEFL 閱讀技巧：遇到「主詞，同位語，動詞」的結構，先跳過逗號內的同位語，找到後面的動詞，再回頭看同位語理解細節。
:::

## 名詞子句（Noun Clauses）

整個子句當名詞用，可以當主詞或受詞。

### that 子句

- Researchers believe **that climate is changing**.
- It is clear **that more funding is needed**.
- Evidence suggests **that the two species shared a common ancestor**.

### whether / if 子句（表達不確定）

- Scientists debated **whether the data were reliable**.
- It remains unclear **whether the treatment works**.
- The study examined **whether sleep deprivation affects decision-making**.

### wh- 子句

- The study explains **why coral reefs are bleaching**.
- We do not yet know **how the virus spreads**.
- Researchers are still investigating **when the first humans arrived on the continent**.

## 副詞子句（Adverb Clauses）

修飾動詞，說明時間、原因、讓步、條件。

### 時間

- **When** the temperature drops, ice forms.
- **After** the experiment was completed, results were analyzed.
- **Once** the data were collected, the team began analysis.
- **Before** drawing conclusions, the researchers reviewed all available evidence.

### 原因

- **Because** the sample was too small, the results were inconclusive.
- **Since** resources were limited, they focused on key variables.
- **As** the population grew, demand for resources increased sharply.

### 讓步（儘管…）

- **Although** the theory was widely accepted, new evidence challenged it.
- **Even though** costs were high, the project continued.
- **While** some species adapted to the changing climate, others did not survive.

### 條件

- **If** the temperature rises, the ice will melt.
- **Unless** action is taken, the situation will worsen.
- **Provided that** the funding is secured, the study will begin in January.

## 平行結構（Parallel Structure）

連接詞（and / or / but）連接的成分必須**詞性相同**。

- ✓ The study is **accurate**, **thorough**, and **well-documented**.
- ✗ The study is **accurate**, **thorough**, and **well documented it**.

- ✓ She enjoys **reading**, **writing**, and **conducting** research.
- ✗ She enjoys **reading**, **writing**, and **to conduct** research.

- ✓ The results were **consistent**, **reproducible**, and **statistically significant**.
- ✗ The results were **consistent**, **reproducible**, and showed **statistical significance**.

`not only...but also` 也要對稱：

- ✓ Not only **did they collect** data, but they also **analyzed** it.
- ✗ Not only **collecting** data, but also they **analyzed** it.
- ✓ Not only **is the method** cost-effective, but it also **produces** accurate results.

## 分詞片語（Participle Phrases）

分詞片語（-ing 或 p.p. 開頭）放在句首時，它修飾的對象**必須是主要子句的主詞**。

如果邏輯上做這個動作的不是主詞，就叫做「懸垂修飾語」（dangling modifier），是常見文法錯誤。

- ✓ **Having reviewed the data**, the team published the results.（team 審查了資料）
- ✗ **Having reviewed the data**, the results were published.（results 沒辦法「審查」資料）

- ✓ **Collected from three sites**, the samples were analyzed in the lab.（samples 被收集）
- ✗ **Collected from three sites**, the researchers analyzed the samples.（researchers 不是被收集的）

- ✓ **Working in remote areas**, researchers often face communication challenges.（researchers 在遠地工作）
- ✓ **Facing a tight deadline**, the team worked through the night.（team 面對截止日）
- ✗ **Facing a tight deadline**, the experiment had to be rushed.（experiment 無法「面對」截止日）

> 遇到句首分詞片語，立刻找主要子句的主詞，問：「是這個主詞在做（或被做）這個動作嗎？」

## 句子類型（Sentence Types）

### 簡單句（Simple Sentence）

一個獨立子句，有一個主詞和一個動詞：

- Scientists discovered a new species.
- The glacier retreated by 300 meters over the past decade.
- High levels of carbon dioxide trap heat in the atmosphere.

### 複合句（Compound Sentence）

兩個獨立子句，用協調連接詞（for / and / nor / but / or / yet / so）連接，中間加逗號：

- The experiment succeeded, **but** the sample size was small.
- Resources were limited, **so** the team had to prioritize.
- The hypothesis was compelling, **yet** the evidence remained inconclusive.
- Scientists identified the problem, **and** they developed a solution within two years.

### 複雜句（Complex Sentence）

一個獨立子句 + 一個（或多個）從屬子句，從屬子句由從屬連接詞引導：

- **Although** the results were promising, further testing is required.
- Scientists believe **that** the climate is changing **because** CO₂ levels are rising.
- **When** the data were analyzed, the team discovered an unexpected pattern.

### 複合複雜句（Compound-Complex Sentence）

包含至少兩個獨立子句和至少一個從屬子句：

- **Because** the data were incomplete, the team revised their model, **and** they repeated the experiment.
- **Although** funding was scarce, the researchers persevered, **and** their work eventually received international recognition.

## 常見句子錯誤

### 句子碎片（Fragment）

缺少主詞、動詞，或只有從屬子句而沒有主句，不能獨立成句：

- ✗ **Because the sample was too small.**（只有從屬子句，沒有主句）
- ✓ **Because the sample was too small, the results were inconclusive.**

- ✗ **The theory proposed by Darwin in 1859.**（沒有動詞）
- ✓ **The theory proposed by Darwin in 1859 revolutionized biology.**

- ✗ **A region where biodiversity is especially high.**（沒有主句動詞）
- ✓ **A region where biodiversity is especially high requires strong protection.**

### 黏合句（Run-on Sentence）

兩個獨立子句沒有適當連接，僅用逗號（或什麼都不加）連接：

- ✗ The experiment failed, the team tried again.（逗號拼接，comma splice）
- ✓ The experiment failed; **the team tried again.**（用分號）
- ✓ The experiment failed, **so the team tried again.**（用連接詞）
- ✓ The experiment failed. **The team tried again.**（分成兩句）

- ✗ The results were significant they were published immediately.（沒有任何連接）
- ✓ The results were significant, **and they were published immediately.**

:::tip
記住：兩個獨立子句之間，只能用「句號 / 分號 / 連接詞 + 逗號」來連接。單獨一個逗號不夠！
:::
